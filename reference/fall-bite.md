# Late-October / Early-November Bite at Topsail

Checked: 2026-10-14. Two parts: TYPICAL (prior-year reports and DMF biology, year stated on every line) and THIS YEAR (October 2026 only).
Main local source: Fisherman's Post monthly "Topsail/Sneads Ferry" report, which collects notes from East Coast Sports (Surf City), the three piers, and local guides (Spring Tide Guide Service, Speckled Specialist Charters, Native Son Guide Service, Plan 9 Charters, South End Adventures, Atlantic Sportfishing, Carolina Flats Fishing).
Reminder for the writer: flounder are CATCH-AND-RELEASE ONLY in the window, and speckled trout have a 14-20" slot (regs.md).

## TYPICAL for this time of year

### Surf and piers
- Nov 1, 2025 report: small pushes of spot arriving with sea mullet (kingfish/whiting), croakers and some reds; bluefish "truly all over the place" (surf, nearshore, ICW); a 28" blue from the surf at south Topsail on a live finger mullet. Spanish thinning out and "likely leaving". https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2025/
- Nov 1, 2025, Jolly Roger Pier: bluefish strong early; bottom fishing better in the evening; bloodworms producing spot, Virginia mullet, sheepshead, pompano and slot reds; big drum at night. Same URL.
- Nov 1, 2025, Surf City Pier: overnight bottom fishing for spot, croaker, mullet, pufferfish; also pompano, bluefish, occasional Spanish, slot reds. Same URL.
- Nov 1, 2025, Seaview Pier: casting plugs around bait for Spanish and small blues; bottom catches of spot, sea mullet, croaker, sheepshead, black drum, speckled trout, red drum. Same URL.
- Nov 1, 2024 report: sea mullet strong at all three piers (Jolly Roger: better after dark); scattered spot; pompano good at Jolly Roger (3-4 lb) and Surf City (2+ lb); bluefish on Gotcha plugs; occasional reds and trout starting at Jolly Roger and Seaview; king mackerel to 36+ lb off the end of Seaview; 33" red drum from the surf on Lea-Hutaff Island (live finger mullet). https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2024/
- Oct 1, 2025 report (early-Oct baseline): Jolly Roger evening reds (upper- and over-slot), Virginia mullet, spot, croaker, blues, black drum, kings to 35 lb; Surf City Pier blues, Spanish to 3.5 lb, spot, pompano, over-slot reds; Seaview blues, Spanish, reds, a few trout early and late, sea mullet, spot, black drum, sheepshead. Seaview noted kings were waiting for water below 76°F. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-october-2025/
- Surf City Pier's own seasonal chart: spot May-Nov, sheepshead Apr-Nov; king and Spanish May-Oct; albacore and blowfish "Fall"; speckled trout "Spring & Fall"; bluefish, croaker, gray trout, red drum, sea mullet "Spring to Fall". https://www.surfcityoceanpier.com/fishing-seasons.html (undated chart)
- Spot (DMF species profile): "As autumn and spawning season draws near, larger spot move into the nearshore ocean." Caught from piers, bridges, jetties, surf on "two-hook bottom rigs baited with shrimp, bloodworms or squid"; typical 7-10". https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries/public-information-and-education/species-profiles/spot (profile page is old; no date shown)
- Baits named across these reports for surf/pier bottom fishing: bloodworms, fresh shrimp, sand fleas, Fishbites, cut bait; live finger mullet for reds and big blues; Gotcha plugs and diamond jigs for blues/Spanish. (Oct 2025, Nov 2024, Nov 2025 reports above)

### Inlet (New Topsail Inlet, New River Inlet) and sound/ICW/marsh
- Nov 1, 2025: migratory speckled trout 16-21" staging around the inlet; resident fish moving into upriver creeks; early-season trout slow but improving. Topwater early; soft plastics on 3/16 oz jig heads, MirrOlures, Vudu shrimp, Rapala X-Raps later; lighter heads upriver, heavier near the inlet where current is stronger; live shrimp under a slip cork "remains a top producer". Hard north winds pushed trout to wintering areas earlier than normal. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2025/
- Nov 1, 2025: red drum solid, many small fish plus some 25-28" upper-slot; red on a finger mullet / Carolina rig in the ICW near Hampstead; black drum on drifted shrimp, best around Sneads Ferry. Same URL.
- Nov 1, 2024: reds starting to "school up" on grass banks and creeks; reds on docks and marsh backs with over-slots mixed in; trout on shoreline current rips, ICW banks moving toward creeks, 6-7 ft holes in the river; sheepshead on pilings with live fiddler crabs; flounder around docks while targeting reds. Trout colors: chartreuse in dirty river water; white, green or gold flake near the ICW and inlets. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2024/
- Oct 1, 2025: flounder biting inside the inlets; trout moving toward the inlets, try 6-8 ft drop-offs; trout strong up the New River; reds around docks, grass banks, shallow bays, creek backs; 30" red in the ICW behind Topsail; black drum mainly on live shrimp. Calm mornings help topwater for reds and trout. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-october-2025/
- Dec 17, 2025 (what followed): trout most active in low light, near inlets or in creeks off the New River; inshore water "still holding in the 60s"; reds at creek mouths, docks, grass banks and expected to school in the shallows. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-december-2025/

### Nearshore (inside ~15 miles)
- False albacore: Nov 1, 2025 "picking up near the beach"; sight-cast diamond jigs at bait schools with a quick, steady retrieve; small epoxy jigs, 007 diamond jigs and flies around bait at New River Inlet. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2025/
- False albacore: Nov 1, 2024 "thick off the beaches around bait pods"; outside the inlet and along the beachfronts. https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-november-2024/
- False albacore timing elsewhere (for context only, not Topsail): a Wilmington-area guide called the Wrightsville run "two to three weeks in October"; Cape Lookout peaks late Oct-Nov. Search summaries of saltmagazinenc.com and coastalreview.org (2024); not opened. LOW confidence.
- King mackerel: Nov 2025 best 10-15 miles with a chance along the beachfront; schoolies to 20+ lb on nearshore structure, slow-trolled live/dead bait. Nov 2024 "on fire", from just outside the inlet to 10-15 miles. Same two URLs.
- Gray trout (weakfish) on nearshore structure by jigging (Nov 2025); big red drum migrating along the nearshore bottom (Nov 2025). Black sea bass move shallower as water cools (Oct and Nov 2025).

### Mullet run
- DMF: striped mullet live in fresh to estuarine water "until migrating to the ocean to spawn in the fall" and "spawn in large aggregations near inlets to offshore areas"; "peak spawning activity occurs from October to early December". NCDMF Striped Mullet FMP Update, August 2024: https://www.deq.nc.gov/marine-fisheries/fisheries-management/annual-fmp-review/2023/2023-striped-mullet-fmp-review/open
- Triggers (northeast winds, strong cold fronts, falling pressure; warm falls or hurricanes can delay the run): from search summaries of DMF mullet documents. UNVERIFIED by direct read.
- Local link: Oct 1, 2026 Fisherman's Post says "the mullet migration is starting" (see THIS YEAR). Live finger mullet on Carolina rigs is the most-named red drum bait in every fall report above.

### Tide, wind, light (only where a source says so)
- Spanish pushing inside the inlet on a high tide (Oct 2026 report, Spring Tide Guide Service).
- Flounder on paddle-tail plastics or live finger mullet on rising tides near creek mouths; at high tide pitch to grass lines and oyster beds (Wrightsville Beach report, Oct 1, 2026): https://www.fishermanspost.com/wrightsville-beach-october-2026/
- Sheepshead/black drum on docks and pilings with moving current; mud crabs preferred for sheepshead (same Wrightsville report).
- Calm mornings = better topwater for reds/trout (Oct 2025). Hard north wind = trout move to wintering areas early (Nov 2025). Pier bottom fishing better in the evening/after dark at Jolly Roger (Nov 2024, Nov 2025).
- No source found that ties the surf bite to a specific tide stage at Topsail. Do not state one as fact.

## THIS YEAR SO FAR (October 2026)

### Fisherman's Post "Topsail/Sneads Ferry - October 2026" (posted Thu Oct 1, 2026)
URL: https://www.fishermanspost.com/topsail-sneads-ferry-october-2026/ . Contributors: East Coast Sports, Native Son Guide Service, Spring Tide Guide Service, Speckled Specialist Charters, Carolina Flats Fishing, Plan 9 Charters, South End Adventures, Jolly Roger Pier, Surf City Pier, Seaview Pier.
- Surf/piers: slot reds with some over-slots, reds from the piers at night; Spanish plentiful from beach and piers with bigger fish showing; bluefish plentiful; king activity starting at the piers, fish to 32 lb at Jolly Roger; also black drum, pompano, Virginia mullet, flounder, sea mullet, spot, croaker, sheepshead, some speckled trout.
- Sound/ICW/creeks: red drum the main target, scattered from ICW and creeks to the inlets, "more schooled in the bays than usual" (Spring Tide); reds at creek mouths, grass lines, docks (Native Son). Trout quiet: some early-morning topwater fish and fish in 15-20 ft on live shrimp under slip corks (Speckled Specialist), others say the bite hasn't started. Flounder mostly bycatch; a 24" flounder from a Hampstead-area dock. Black drum 3-4 lb around bridges and oyster reefs.
- Nearshore: Spanish just outside the inlet in 15-20 ft (Plan 9) and under birds at 45-50 ft (South End Adventures); kings 8-12 miles, nearshore king bite slow, most in 50-60 ft; false albacore "expected soon", some already mixed with Spanish on Plan 9's grounds.
- Baits/lures: live finger mullet on Carolina rigs; Gulp under popping corks; Heddon Spook Jr. topwater early; live shrimp under slip corks; cut bait for reds some days; Gotcha plugs and diamond jigs for Spanish/blues; small metal/epoxy jigs for sight-casting; trolled spoons off the beach.
- Outlook in the report: trout should improve as water cools; the mullet migration is starting.

### Fisherman's Post "Wrightsville Beach - October 2026" (posted Oct 1, 2026; ~25 mi south, for context)
URL: https://www.fishermanspost.com/wrightsville-beach-october-2026/
- Surf: small sharks; pompano, sea mullet, scattered reds on bottom rigs; Spanish/blues better from piers; pompano bite improving; kings to 33 lb starting to show. Nearshore: Spanish 1-3 mi; false albacore arriving 5-8 mi. Trout slow overall.

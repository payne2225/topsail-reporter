## Text
New moon Monday: sunny, 71, a west wind that fades through the day. Work topwater over the creeks off Banks Channel around the 7:48 AM sound high, then follow the water out: the marsh is off the grass 10:56 AM to 4:11 PM, so fish the drains and oyster edges with cut mullet or Gulp for reds, shrimp under a cork in the deeper seams for trout. The moon is taking the night off, so the tide is doing the heavy lifting.

## Lead
A new moon tide and a warm, fading west wind make Monday a marsh day: the sound tops out at 7:48 AM and the grass stands clear from 10:56 AM to 4:11 PM. Sunny and 71, clear and 47 tonight, seas 2 to 3 feet.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The marsh drains and oyster edges north of the inlet from 10:56 AM, when the water leaves the grass, to the 2:29 PM low, with cut mullet or Gulp for reds.

### The sound
- **When:** High 7:48 AM, ebb to the 2:29 PM low, flood to 8:02 PM. Out of the grass 10:56 AM to 4:11 PM. New moon: hard current.
- **Where:** Creek mouths off Banks Channel at the top of the tide, then the drains and oyster banks as the marsh empties; docks on the dusk flood.
- **Bait:** Topwater early, then live shrimp under a cork in 4 to 8 feet or a 3/16 oz jig; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; black drum to 18; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

### The inlet
- **When:** Ebb from the 6:59 AM ocean high; the west wind flattens the bar, so watch for birds.
- **Latest:** Bluefish off the beach, false albacore when bait shows; sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Falling to the 1:24 PM low, flood to 7:16 PM; offshore wind over a 3 foot east swell, clean faces.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha at first light for blues.
- **Latest:** Occasional specks here; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
The current runs hardest halfway between high and low, so add weight or move up a creek until your cork drifts slowly. Near the 2:29 PM low, lighten up again.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **Oysters:** In season: hand harvest only, 3 inch minimum, a bushel a person, daylight only, no license. Topsail and Stump Sounds are at normal boundaries; check the closure map before you pick.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational since SF-2-2026 (Nov 5, commercial oyster dredging). No new shellfish closure; hotline 252-515-5656.

## Things To Do
- **Shells at the 1:24 PM low.** A spring low still bares the wide sand; walk the tide line for black triangles. The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is quiet on a Monday; dogs leashed there. Kids love it.
- **Breakfast on the water.** Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave, Surf City, opens 8 AM seven days, breakfast menu to 10:30, docks on the waterway. White Crab and the Jolly Roger grill are closed Mondays.
- **Ride the island.** 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S Topsail Dr, Surf City, 10 AM to 6 PM today, e-bikes and cruisers; Herring's, 701 N New River Dr, 9 AM to 8 PM, cruiser $20 for four hours. Little wind by afternoon.
- **A pier at sunrise.** Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S Shore Dr, open 24 hours, a dollar to walk out; the sun comes up over the ocean at 6:38 AM and the grill pours coffee from 7.
- **Wilmington day trip.** Battleship North Carolina, daily 8 AM to 5 PM, $14 adults, $6 ages 6 to 11; Wilmington Railroad Museum, 505 Nutt St, 10 AM to 4 PM, $12, kids $8. Cape Fear Museum is closed today. About 50 minutes.
- **Sunset on the sound.** Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, boardwalk and pirate-ship playground, free parking; the sun sets over the water at 5:10 PM under a clear sky.

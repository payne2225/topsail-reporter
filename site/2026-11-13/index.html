Front's through: trout on the dawn flood, reds on the marsh drains from 1:29 PM

The clear, cool day behind the front: sunny and 62, west around 12 with gusts to 18, no advisory. Trout on the flood to the 10:41 AM sound high, then the marsh is off the grass from 1:29 PM through the 5:08 PM low.

The Bite

Best bet: The marsh drains north of the inlet from 1:29 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 5:08 PM sound low, cut mullet on a Carolina rig for reds.

The sound

  • WhenFlood to the 10:41 AM high, ebb to the 5:08 PM low. Out of the grass 1:29 PM to 8:42 PM.
  • WhereDeep seams, 5 to 8 feet, in the main creeks off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster bars once the marsh empties.
  • BaitLive shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig with a soft plastic for trout; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer is posted.

The inlet

  • WhenFlood to the 9:52 AM inlet high, ebb to the 5:11 PM low; seas 2 to 4 feet.
  • LatestBluefish, false albacore and gray trout on nearshore bait, diamond or epoxy jigs (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFlood to the 9:35 AM high, ebb to the 4:09 PM low; offshore wind, waves near 2 feet, water 68.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for whiting and pompano.
  • LatestSlot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues at first light, bottom fish after dark; no pier lists a closing date.
  • LatestReds past 32 inches at the north end; Jolly Roger blues early, sea mullet and spot at night; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

After a cold night, trout slide into the deepest bend of a creek. Let your jig touch bottom, then lift and drop it slowly; most bites come on the fall.

Beach & Weather

  • TonightMostly clear and 40. Saturday sunny and 56, then 35 Saturday night; WWAY says frost is likely and a freeze possible inland Saturday night and Sunday morning.

  • WearFleece and a knit hat at dawn, a windbreaker by noon, gloves for the night bite.

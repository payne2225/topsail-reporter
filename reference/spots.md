Checked: 2026-10-14. Hours and fees change; phone numbers are given so the routine can tell readers to call.

## Ocean piers (all three report monthly through Fisherman's Post; see sources.md)

### Jolly Roger Pier (Topsail Beach)
- Address: 803 Ocean Blvd., Topsail Beach, NC 28445 (next to the Jolly Roger Inn). Phone 910-328-4616; toll-free 800-633-3196. https://www.thejollyroger.com/
- 2026 ticket prices (page heading "2026 Pier Ticket Prices"): Fishing daily $16, 3-day $44, 5-day $69, weekly $93, season $300. King ticket daily $27, 3-day $75, 5-day $109, weekly $149, season $400. Spectator $2/day. Kids 13 and under half-price fishing. Fishing ticket up to 2 rods; King ticket up to 3 rods. https://www.thejollyroger.com/index.php?c=pier
- Blanket CRFL: "The pier maintains a Blanket Coastal Recreational Fishing License that covers customers while they fish on the pier." Same URL.
- Hours: page says tackle shop is "open early and late" and pier is "fully lit at night". Owner quoted in 2016: "We're open 24/7 in season." https://www.visitpender.com/blog/the-jolly-roger-pier-is-perfect-for-fishing-picture-taking-and-dolphin-watching (dated Feb 2016; old)
- Open Oct 29-Nov 7, 2026? LIKELY OPEN, NOT CONFIRMED. No 2026 closing date is published. Evidence it fishes in early November: Fisherman's Post Nov 1, 2025 and Nov 1, 2024 reports include Jolly Roger catches (fall-bite.md). Call 910-328-4616 to confirm.
- Fishing report on own site: STALE. Latest entry 6/1/25. https://www.thejollyroger.com/index.php?c=fishing
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/JollyRogerInnandPier (not fetchable; see sources.md)
- NCDMF Saltwater Fishing Tournament weigh station (list file dated 8.4.26). https://deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/marine-fisheries/public-information-and-education/coastal-fishing-information/nc-saltwater-fishing-tournament/official-weigh-stations

### Surf City Ocean Pier (Surf City)
- Address: 112 South Shore Drive, Surf City, NC 28445. Phone (910) 328-3521. https://www.surfcityoceanpier.com/
- Hours: "The Pier is Open 24hrs a day 7 days a week including holidays." Grill/ice cream: Sun-Thu 7am-10pm, Fri-Sat 7am-midnight. Same URL.
- Daily fees ("2026/2027 Daily Fishing Fees"): $12 one rod or $24 two rods, ticket good 24 hours from purchase; child pass (10 and under) $6; crab trap $12; cast net $12 "when allowed"; King $36 (3 rods) or $48 (4 rods), day only; spectator $1 (age 11+). https://www.surfcityoceanpier.com/season-passes--fees.html
- Blanket license: "You DO NOT need a NC State License to fish from Surf City Pier" / "The Surf City Pier has a Blanket License to cover persons fishing from the pier." Same URL.
- Open Oct 29-Nov 7, 2026? YES (high confidence). Fees page: carts/coolers/equipment must be removed by November 30; early 2027 pass pricing runs to 11/30/26. A local guide listing says the season runs "from mid-March to the end of November": https://topsailguide.com/profile/surf-city-ocean-pier/
- Fishing report on own site: NONE dated. Home page "What's Currently Biting" just points to the Events tab. Has live cams (/live-cams.html) and a seasonal species chart (/fishing-seasons.html). Daily catches go on its Facebook page (per visitpender article, undated).
- Pier's own species chart (fishing-seasons.html): sheepshead Apr-Nov, spot May-Nov, king and Spanish mackerel May-Oct, speckled trout "Spring & Fall", albacore and blowfish "Fall", bluefish/croaker/gray trout/red drum/sea mullet "Spring to Fall". https://www.surfcityoceanpier.com/fishing-seasons.html
- Weigh station (same DMF list).

## New Topsail Inlet / south end ("The Point", Serenity Point, now "Topsail Nature Preserve")
- The south-end tract fronting the ocean, New Topsail Inlet and Banks Channel transferred to the state; NC DEQ Division of Coastal Management manages it as the Topsail Nature Preserve with the NC Coastal Land Trust. Transfer event May 19, 2026. https://www.deq.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/05/14/deq-secretary-reid-wilson-join-staff-officials-and-conservation-partners-mark-transfer-topsail-beach
- Deed records show just over 97 acres transferred (earlier materials said ~150). Preserve "remains open for walking, fishing, beachcombing, and wildlife watching." Stay out of marked nesting areas; pack out fishing line. https://topsailguide.com/news/point-protected-topsail-beach-south-end/ (published May 20, 2026; updated July 17, 2026)
- Town notice 9/11/2026 "Protecting the South End": vehicular beach driving, horseback riding and dogs off leash no longer permitted on the preserve beach strand; beach driving and horseback permits no longer issued or renewed; dogs on leash at all times at the South End; pedestrian access stays open for anglers. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1242/Protecting-the-South-End-Updates-to-Beach-Driving,-Horseback-Riding,-and-Pet-Guidelines
- Preserve closed Oct 7, 2026 for phragmites herbicide treatment and has REOPENED (town news item dated 10/7/2026, "Topsail Nature Preserve is now OPEN!"). https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1250/Topsail-Nature-Preserve---Reopened
- Getting there: DEQ's event notice had people walk to the oceanside beach from the Sound Access #1 parking area (= the town's South End Parking Lot). Same DEQ URL.
- Inlet channel surveys (TI Coastal) posted 5/5/2026 and 9/17/2026; findings not summarized on the page. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1243/Inlet-Channel-Survey---Summer-2026
- Bird-nesting closure dates for fall 2026: none found. UNVERIFIED (nesting season closures are normally spring/summer; nothing published for this window).

## Topsail Beach public accesses and parking (south end)
- South End Parking Lot = Sound Access #1: paid "Premium Lot", 6:00 AM-9:00 PM, $7/hr (up to 4 hrs) or $35/day; the only lot closed 10 PM-6 AM; no overnight parking. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/Visitors/Public-Accesses-and-Parking
- Ocean-front lots (Accesses #5-#15): $6/hr (up to 4 hrs) or $30/day, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM. Same URL.
- Paid parking "Enforced every day, March 1st through October 31st." So paid parking ends after Sat Oct 31; Nov 1-7 not enforced (page implies, does not say "free"). Same URL.
- Fines: $50 standard; same-day pay $30 (ocean lots) / $35 (South End lot). Same URL.
- Full list of beach and sound accesses is a linked spreadsheet ("Beach and Sound Accesses List") on the same page; not opened.

## Beach driving (window-relevant)
- Topsail Beach: no more beach-driving permits for the South End / preserve (Sept 11, 2026 notice above). Historically the town allowed 4WD Oct 1-Jan 15 (topsailguide search summary; superseded for the south end by the 2026 notice).
- Surf City: beach driving not allowed (2020 WECT report; current status UNVERIFIED). https://www.wect.com/2020/10/29/topsail-beach-surf-city-not-allowing-beach-driving

Town page: access requires 4WD, 5 mph, no ATVs/motorcycles, park inside the access. https://www.northtopsailbeachnc.gov/215/Beach-Rules-Activities . Permit rules/season: UNVERIFIED (town 4x4 page node/2459 returned 404). Town Hall 910-328-1349 (search result).

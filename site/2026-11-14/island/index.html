Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny and 58 after 42 at sunrise, northwest 9 to 13 with gusts to 21; north 15 to 20 knots on the water early, easing to northwest 10 to 15 after noon.
  • TonightClear and 37. Sunday sunny and 58 again, northwest around 8.
  • SurfShort-period chop 1 to 3 feet, water 67, the wind blowing offshore. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; wade knee deep.
  • WearFleece, hat and gloves at dawn; a windbreaker by noon.

Things To Do

  • Breakfast on the south end. White Crab Coffee and Books, 708 S Anderson Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM Saturdays, coffee and baked goods. The Jolly Roger Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, breakfast sandwiches 7 AM to 2 PM. Both suit kids.
  • Or breakfast up the island. Beach Bums, 4021 Island Dr, North Topsail Beach, breakfast 8 to 11 AM Saturdays. The Surf City Pier grill opens at 7 AM; a $1 spectator pass walks you out over a flat ocean.
  • Chili in Burgaw. NC Blueberry Festival Chili Cook-Off, Courthouse Ave, Burgaw, 11 AM to 3 PM. Taste and vote; votes in by 2 PM. Outdoors, so dress for 58. Kids welcome.
  • A free festival or a holiday market. Amigos International Festival, Ogden Park, Wilmington, 11 AM to 8 PM, free, food priced by the stall. CAMmade Holiday Market, Cameron Art Museum, 10 to 3, $5 covers both days.
  • Shells at the 4:55 PM low. Twelve minutes before the 5:07 PM sunset, the offshore wind keeping the water calm. Black triangles on the tide line are shark teeth; the south-end preserve is open on foot, dogs leashed.
  • Candlelight in Swansboro. Downtown Swansboro, 5 to 9 PM: Nutcracker scenes at 5, tree lighting at 6, shops open late. Or The Drifters at Thalian Hall, Wilmington, 7:30 PM, from $53.

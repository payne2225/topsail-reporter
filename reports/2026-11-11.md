## Text
Veterans Day is the last calm one: partly sunny, 72, wind under 10 until evening. Throw topwater over the creek mouths off Banks Channel on the flood to the 9:13 AM sound high, then work the marsh drains once the water is off the grass, 12:12 PM to 7:08 PM, with cut mullet or Gulp for reds and shrimp under a cork for trout. Thursday blows southwest to 30 knots, so get it done today. The bait thieves are expecting you.

## Lead
Wednesday is the last quiet one: partly sunny, 72, a light wind until dark, the sound high at 9:13 AM and the marsh off the grass 12:12 PM to 7:08 PM. Thursday blows southwest to 30 knots and Friday turns cold.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The marsh drains and oyster edges north of the inlet from 12:12 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 3:50 PM sound low, with cut mullet or Gulp for reds.

### The sound
- **When:** High 9:13 AM, ebb to the 3:50 PM low, flood to 9:27 PM. Out of the grass 12:12 PM to 7:08 PM; still a big swing.
- **Where:** Creek mouths off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster banks as the marsh empties; seams 4 to 8 feet for trout.
- **Bait:** A Spook Jr. early; live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in the seams; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

### The inlet
- **When:** Ebb from the 8:32 AM inlet high to the 3:47 PM low; light wind, so watch for birds.
- **Latest:** Bluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait; sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Falling to the 2:45 PM low, flood to 8:32 PM; seas 2 feet on an east swell, water 68.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha early for blues.
- **Latest:** Slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues early, bottom fish after dark; no pier has posted a closing date.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
When the water drops off the grass, reds follow it out through the little drains. Cast up-current of a drain mouth, let the cut mullet sit, and let them come to you.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **Oysters:** In season in open waters; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries, no new closure since Oct 16.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** SF-3-2026 (Nov 10) is commercial oyster dredging in Pamlico Sound; nothing recreational has changed.

## Things To Do
- **Shells at the 2:45 PM low.** A spring low still bares the wide sand; walk the tide line for black triangles. The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is quiet midweek; dogs leashed there. Kids love it.
- **Coffee on the south end.** White Crab Coffee and Books, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM today, books and baked goods. The Patio at the Surf, by the roundabout, serves breakfast from 7 AM; its page also says 6, so call ahead.
- **The Battleship on Veterans Day.** Battleship North Carolina, Wilmington, 8 AM to 5 PM, $14 adults, $6 ages 6 to 11, $10 military; ham radio remembrance 9 AM to 4 PM. Mostly indoors, kids roam nine levels. About 50 minutes.
- **Ride with no headwind.** 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S Topsail Dr, Surf City, 10 AM to 6 PM, e-bikes and cruisers; Herring's, 701 N New River Dr, 9 AM to 8 PM, cruiser $20 for four hours. Wind under 10 all day.
- **Airlie Gardens.** Bird walk 8 to 9:30 AM, then the gardens, 9 AM to 5 PM, $10 adults, $3 ages 4 to 12, open on the holiday per the county. About 45 minutes.
- **Sunset over the sound.** Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, free; the sun goes down at 5:08 PM over the Intracoastal. Kids get the pirate-ship playground.

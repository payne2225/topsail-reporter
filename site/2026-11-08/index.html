The wind quits for the tournament's last morning: topwater trout, then shrimp in the seams

Sunday is the calm after two blowy days: sunny, 66, north breeze going slack by midday and a clear 48 degree night. The spring tide tops out in the sound at 7:05 AM, so fish the creeks at dawn and the falling water after.

The Bite

Best bet: Topwater over the creek mouths off Banks Channel until the 7:05 AM sound high, then live shrimp under a cork in 4 to 8 foot seams on the ebb.

The sound

  • WhenHigh 7:05 AM, ebb to 1:44 PM, flood to the 7:19 PM high. New moon tomorrow: hard current.
  • WhereCreek mouths and grass lines early, deeper seams as the sun climbs, docks on the dusk flood for reds. Bush Marina's ramp: call 910-328-2746 first.
  • BaitA Spook Jr. at dawn, then live shrimp under a slip cork or a 3/16 oz jig and plastic; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; black drum to 18; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The inlet

  • WhenEbb from the 6:20 AM ocean high; calm and sunny, so watch for surface bait.
  • LatestGood bluefish off the beach, false albacore possible on sight-cast jigs (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFalling to the 12:43 PM low, flood to 6:36 PM; seas 3 to 4 feet, cleaner as the wind goes northwest.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook bottom rig for sea mullet, pompano and black drum; a Gotcha plug for blues.
  • LatestOccasional specks here; slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues at first light, bottom fish after dark. Tournament lines out at noon.
  • BaitBloodworms or shrimp on the bottom; Gotcha plugs early.
  • LatestJolly Roger scattered specks, night mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

When a trout swirls behind your topwater and misses, keep walking it; stopping the plug ends the chase. Keep a second rod rigged with shrimp for the same spot.

Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny and 66 after a 44 degree dawn; north wind 5 to 7 early, calm by afternoon, then light west.
  • TonightClear and 48, west wind 3 to 7. Monday sunny and 70.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet on a 10 second east swell, waves to 3 at the beach, water 69. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; wade knee deep on a spring tide.
  • WearFleece for the morning, a t-shirt by lunch, sunglasses all day.

Regs Corner

  • Speckled trout14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
  • Red drum18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
  • FlounderClosed; release every one.
  • Report itAny kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
  • New from NCDMFNothing recreational. SF-2-2026 (Nov 5) covers commercial mechanical oyster harvest from Nov 16. No new shellfish closure since Oct 16; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries. Hotline 252-515-5656.

The Last Cast

Two days of north wind, and it finally takes Sunday off, right as the tournament clock runs out. Timing, as the trout say, is everything.

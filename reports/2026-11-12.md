## Lead
Thursday is the wind day: sunny and 74, southwest 12 to 18 with gusts near 30, a Small Craft Advisory on the water. The sound high is 9:56 AM, the marsh off the grass 12:50 PM to 7:54 PM; Friday turns cold.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The marsh drains north of the inlet from 12:50 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 4:28 PM sound low, cut mullet on a Carolina rig for reds, in a creek bend out of the wind.

### The sound
- **When:** Flood to the 9:56 AM high, ebb to the 4:28 PM low. Out of the grass 12:50 PM to 7:54 PM; dawn is the calmer window.
- **Where:** Creek mouths and deep seams off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster banks once the marsh empties.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in 4 to 8 feet for trout; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **When:** Ebb from the 9:11 AM inlet high to the 4:28 PM low, on foot from the Point; seas 4 to 6 feet keep boats home.
- **Latest:** Bluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait, sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Flood to the 8:54 AM high, ebb to the 3:26 PM low. Waves to 3 feet, water 68.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano.
- **Latest:** Slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues early, bottom fish after dark; no pier has posted a closing date.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
A wind on your casting shoulder wraps a cork rig around the rod tip. Turn so it blows across the other one, cast low, and let the current do the rest.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Sheepshead 14 inches, five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **False albacore:** No state size or bag limit; NCDMF's rule page says "no management changes are authorized" this year.
- **Oysters:** In season in open waters; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries, no closure since Oct 16.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing since SF-3-2026 (Nov 10), commercial oyster dredging in Pamlico Sound; nothing recreational has changed.

## Island Lead

## Things To Do
- **Shells at the 3:26 PM low.** Walk northeast up the beach with the southwest wind at your back and the tide line bared; black triangles are shark teeth. The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is open on foot, dogs leashed. Kids love it.
- **Breakfast in North Topsail.** Beach Bums, 4021 Island Dr, serves breakfast 8 to 11 AM Thursdays. The Patio at the Surf, by the Surf City roundabout, opens at 7 Monday to Saturday; its page also says 8, so call ahead.
- **Dig for shark teeth on the mainland.** Surf City Rocks, 22270 US 17, Hampstead, 10 AM to 6 PM: buy a bucket, sift it in the trough, keep the fossils and a geode. About 20 minutes; built for kids.
- **A pier walk and coffee at sunrise.** Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S Shore Dr, is open 24 hours; the sun is up at 6:41 AM and the grill opens at 7. Spectator pass $1, ages 11 and up.
- **Art and a film after dark.** Cameron Art Museum, Wilmington, open until 9 PM Thursdays, $17 adults, $6 ages 6 to 17; Live! at CAM Cafe 6 to 8 PM, then a film screening. About 50 minutes.
- **Ballet tonight.** American Ballet Theatre Studio Company, Thalian Hall, Wilmington, 7:30 PM. Or stay close for the 5:08 PM sunset over the sound at Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, free, with the pirate-ship playground for kids.

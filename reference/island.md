## 2. Attractions on the island

### Sea turtle hospital
- Karen Beasley Sea Turtle Rescue and Rehabilitation Center, 302 Tortuga Lane, Surf City (mainland side, off Charlie Medlin Dr past the roundabout, gravel end of Tortuga Lane); 910-329-0222. Guided tours ONLY, reservations required, 15 tickets per slot, "We SELL OUT daily!" Page says "NO PUBLIC TOURS IN OCTOBER" and lists tour dates "Friday, November 27th - Sunday November 29th" and "Saturday, December 5th & Saturday, December 12th". So NO public tours Nov 5-14, 2026 (re-read 2026-10-23, unchanged). Admission when open: $7 general (13-61), $6 military/veterans, $6 seniors 62+, $5 ages 3-12, free 0-2 (ticket still required), plus 6.75% tax and $0.31 fee. "The Gift Shop is open during tour hours" (so not open in the window on that wording). Do not park in the Surf City Pet Hospital lot. HIGH. https://www.seaturtlehospital.org/visit/ (read 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-23)

HIGH. https://missilesandmoremuseum.org/museum-hours/ ; https://topsailhistoricalsociety.org/
- Self-guided "Historical Top Secret Walking Tour of Topsail Beach": about one mile, self-guided (stops not listed on the home page). MED. https://topsailhistoricalsociety.org/
- Operation Bumblebee observation towers (free to see from public roads and beaches; some are PRIVATE HOMES, do not trespass): NC highway marker D-104 "Missile Tests" stands at NC 50 (Anderson Blvd) at Flake Ave, Topsail Beach; text: "Observation towers line Topsail Island; Assembly Building 2 blocks west." The marker page says seven of the original eight towers still stand; some are private homes, one is part of a fishing pier. HIGH. https://dncr.nc.gov/blog/2023/12/11/missile-tests-d-104 (read 2026-10-16)
- Observation Tower No. 2 ("Queen's Grant Tower"), 1000 block S. Anderson Blvd, Topsail Beach; listed on the National Register. MED (Wikipedia, search summary). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Naval_Ordnance_Testing_Facility_Observation_Tower_No._2

## 3. Beach things

### Shark teeth and shells
- Topsail is named among North Carolina's best shark-tooth beaches (with Holden Beach and Ocean Isle) by Walter Magazine and Beachcombing Magazine; Visit Pender: "Walk our peaceful, uncrowded beaches where finding shark teeth is a favorite pastime." MED. https://waltermagazine.com/explore/best-north-carolina-beaches-for-shark-teet/ (search summary) ; https://www.visitpender.com/blog/why-we-love-topsail-island (search summary)
- When and where (general NC-coast advice, not Topsail-specific): "Low tide is usually better and places like sandbars, where water constantly shifts the sand, tend to be hot spots"; new finds follow disturbed sand "Whenever there's beach replenishment, or the ocean floor gets dredged ... or after a storm"; look along the tideline for small triangles, most often BLACK fossil teeth; a proper shark-tooth sifter beats a kitchen sieve. Garden & Gun, July 27, 2018. MED (old, general). https://gardenandgun.com/secrets-finding-shark-teeth (read 2026-10-16)
- Go early: dawn or the first low tide beats other hunters (Cardinal Pine; Our State essay on Topsail). LOW (search summaries). https://cardinalpine.com/community/best-spots-to-hunt-for-shark-teeth-in-north-carolina/ ; https://www.ourstate.com/the-find-of-a-lifetime/
- Daily low-tide times for the window are in the sources.md almanac (ocean station 8657419). Daylight lows from that almanac (recomputed 2026-10-23): one per day, sliding later each day: Nov 5 10:17 AM, Nov 6 11:10 AM, Nov 7 11:58 AM, Nov 8 12:43 PM, Nov 9 1:24 PM, Nov 10 2:05 PM, Nov 11 2:45 PM, Nov 12 3:26 PM, Nov 13 4:09 PM, Nov 14 4:55 PM (12 minutes before the 5:07 PM sunset). The other low each day falls at night (10:27 PM-3:36 AM). Lows are lowest (0.3 ft) Nov 7-9 around the new moon and rise to 0.9-1.0 ft by Nov 14. Re-fetch the NOAA API each day.
- Shelling by boat: Lea-Hutaff Island (no ferry; see section 2 operators). Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is open for "walking, fishing, beachcombing, and wildlife watching" (see spots.md; dogs leashed there at all times).
- Rule for the edition: say "storm" or "after a blow" only if the NWS forecast for the past days shows one; never claim teeth are "washing up" without a source.

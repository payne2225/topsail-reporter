Topsail Reporter, Sat Oct 24

Boats stay in under a Small Craft Advisory with 6 to 8 foot seas, so fish the sound: the Hampstead tide ebbs from the 7:18 AM high to 1:48 PM and floods back to 7:38 PM. Red drum at the creek mouths on live finger mullet or a Gulp shrimp under a popping cork are the call, and the trout are turning on as the water cools. Tuck into the creeks out of a northeast wind that gusts past 30 at dawn and eases by afternoon. Surf 2 to 4 feet with a strong current, so no swimming. Hang on to your hat, and your bait bucket.

Sunny, 71 and windy: a beach-walk Saturday with a midday low tide for shells

Saturday is sunny and cool: 54 degrees at dawn, a high near 71, a northeast wind around 15 to 18 mph with gusts past 30 early and in the low 20s by mid afternoon, and mostly clear tonight with a low around 58. The surf is 2 to 4 feet with a strong current running down the beach and a Moderate rip current risk, so it is a day to walk the beach rather than swim it, and the 12:37 PM low tide on the beach side is the hour for shells and shark teeth. A Coastal Flood Advisory means sound-side streets may be wet around the evening high tide at 7:38 PM. Sunday is sunnier and windier still, then the wind drops Monday and the whole week stays sunny and dry with highs in the mid 70s; bring a jacket for dawn and for sunset at 6:25 PM.

Beach & Weather

Sunny all day, high near 71 after a 54 degree dawn; the northeast wind runs 15 to 18 mph with gusts to 34 at dawn, around 28 through the morning and 21 to 23 by mid afternoon, then back near 29 after dark. Tonight mostly clear, low around 58, northeast 14 to 17 mph gusting 25. Surf 2 to 4 feet, rip current risk Moderate, and a Beach Hazards Statement until 8 PM for a strong longshore current running north to south: it can sweep a swimmer down the beach toward the piers and off their feet, so this is a walking beach today, feet wet at most. Water in the upper 60s in the surf zone and 69 at the buoy. UV index Moderate. A Coastal Flood Advisory runs from this evening until 10 AM Sunday for up to half a foot of water above ground in low spots along the waterway and the tidal creeks around the high tides, 7:38 PM tonight and 8:05 AM Sunday at Hampstead: expect wet sound-side streets and parking spots, and do not drive through water you cannot see the bottom of. The Small Craft Advisory on the coastal waters runs through Monday evening: northeast 20 to 30 knots, gusts near 35, seas up to 10 feet. Long sleeves and a windbreaker for the beach, a real jacket for dawn and for sunset at 6:25 PM.

Sunday sunny and 72, breezier, northeast 16 to 21 mph gusting 31, surf 3 to 5 feet and the rip current risk High; ocean high 7:05 AM, low 1:24 PM, high 7:23 PM; Sunday night 52. Monday sunny and 73 on a north wind 9 to 14 mph, surf 3 to 6 feet, rip risk High, the advisory ending in the evening, and 48 Monday night, the coldest of the week. Tuesday sunny and 73, Wednesday mostly sunny and 74, both with the rip current risk still High and surf 2 to 5 feet. Thursday 75 and Friday 76 and sunny. No rain in the seven day forecast.

Things To Do

  • Shells and shark teeth at the 12:37 PM low tide. The beach-side low is 12:37 PM today (the other low is midnight). The wind is from the northeast, so walk northeast into it first and come home with it at your back. Look along the tideline for small black triangles; a sifter beats a kitchen sieve. The Beach Hazards Statement says the current along the beach is strong today, so keep the kids to ankle depth. Good with kids, and the midday timing suits a late breakfast. Tomorrow's beach low is 1:24 PM.
  • Cheer the Surf to Sound 5K, Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM. The runners cross the Surf City bridge and finish at the park; the race raises money for the sea turtle hospital, which has no public tours this month (its own page). Stroller friendly, no dogs on the course (RunSignup, read today). The park's pirate-ship playground and boardwalk on the waterway are there for the kids afterward.
  • Breakfast, open today (each on its own page, read today). Jolly Roger Pier Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, Topsail Beach, breakfast and lunch sandwiches Friday to Sunday 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM, with the pier and the ocean out the window. Beach Bums Pizzeria, 4021 Island Dr, North Topsail Beach, breakfast 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM on a Saturday. The Patio at the Surf, 104 Charlie Medlin Dr by the roundabout, breakfast and brunch 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM Saturday. Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave on the waterway, breakfast 8:00 AM to 10:30 AM, seven days.
  • Last ice cream of the year at Island Delights, 316 N. New River Dr, Surf City. The 1950s style diner with Hershey's ice cream posted on Facebook on October 23 that this is its last weekend open for the year: Saturday 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, last day Sunday 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM. A fine excuse for a cone with kids before it goes quiet until spring; it may show as closed for the season on the Lunch & Dinner list below, so trust the post, or call.
  • Walk a pier and watch the ocean work. Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S. Shore Dr, is open 24 hours with a $1 spectator ticket for anyone 11 and older, and the grill and ice cream window runs 7:00 AM to midnight on a Saturday (pier site, read today). Jolly Roger Pier, 803 Ocean Blvd, Topsail Beach, is $2 to walk (pier page, October 23). With seas of 6 to 8 feet offshore and the wind up, the end of a pier is the best seat on the island, and it needs a jacket. Good with kids for an hour.
  • Browse, ride, and catch the sunset at 6:25 PM. South End Outfitters, 627 S. Anderson Blvd, Topsail Beach, is open 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM Saturdays, and 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S. Topsail Dr, Surf City, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on its fall hours, with beach cruisers and e-bikes to rent (both own sites, read today; a northeast wind means ride northeast first and coast home). Then the sound side for the sunset: the Soundside Park boardwalk in Surf City, or in North Topsail Beach the observation deck at W. Rodney Knowles Park, 3623 Island Dr, open 8 AM to dusk year round with paid parking (town parks page, October 23). The wind will be at your back there, which is the right way round for a sunset. No rain is forecast, so no indoor fallback is needed today; if the wind drives you inside, Wilmington's Ghostbusters in Concert and the free Sunday fall fests are in Around the Island.

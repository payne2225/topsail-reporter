New moon, warm west wind: fish the marsh as it leaves the grass from 10:53 AM

A new moon tide and a warm, fading west wind make Monday a marsh day: the sound tops out at 7:48 AM and the grass stands clear from 10:53 AM to 5:39 PM. Sunny and 71, clear and 47 tonight, seas 2 to 3 feet.

The Bite

Best bet: The marsh drains and oyster edges north of the inlet from 10:53 AM, when the water leaves the grass, to the 2:29 PM low, with cut mullet or Gulp for reds.

The sound

  • WhenHigh 7:48 AM, ebb to the 2:29 PM low, flood to 8:02 PM. Out of the grass 10:53 AM to 5:39 PM. New moon: hard current.
  • WhereCreek mouths off Banks Channel at the top of the tide, then the drains and oyster banks as the marsh empties; docks on the dusk flood.
  • BaitTopwater early, then live shrimp under a cork in 4 to 8 feet or a 3/16 oz jig; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; black drum to 18; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

The inlet

  • WhenEbb from the 6:59 AM ocean high; the west wind flattens the bar, so watch for birds.
  • LatestBluefish off the beach, false albacore when bait shows; sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFalling to the 1:24 PM low, flood to 7:16 PM; offshore wind over a 3 foot east swell, clean faces.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha at first light for blues.
  • LatestOccasional specks here; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues at first light, bottom fish after dark.
  • LatestJolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

The current runs hardest halfway between high and low, so add weight or move up a creek until your cork drifts slowly. Near the 2:29 PM low, lighten up again.

Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny and 71 after a 48 degree start; west wind near 10 early with gusts near 20, under 10 by afternoon.
  • TonightClear and 47, a light northwest wind. Tuesday sunny and 71 again.
  • SurfSeas 2 to 3 feet on a 3 foot east swell at 11 seconds, water 68. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; a spring low pulls hard off the bars, so wade knee deep.
  • WearFleece and a hat at dawn, shirtsleeves by noon, sunglasses all day: the glare off a flat sound is real.

Regs Corner

  • Speckled trout14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
  • Red drum18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
  • FlounderClosed; release every one.
  • OystersIn season: hand harvest only, 3 inch minimum, a bushel a person, daylight only, no license. Topsail and Stump Sounds are at normal boundaries; check the closure map before you pick.
  • Report itAny kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
  • New from NCDMFNothing recreational since SF-2-2026 (Nov 5, commercial oyster dredging). No new shellfish closure; hotline 252-515-5656.

The Last Cast

A new moon means no moon at all, the one night the fish cannot blame the light. They will think of something.

Sunny, cool and breezy: a beach-walk day with a morning low tide for shells

Tuesday is sunny and cool: 48 degrees at dawn, a high near 74, a north wind around 14 mph gusting to 22, and clear tonight with a low around 52. The surf is up, 3 to 5 feet on a long east swell with a Moderate rip current risk, so it is a day to walk the beach rather than swim it, and the 9:08 AM low tide on the beach side is the hour for shells and shark teeth. The rest of the week stays sunny and dry with highs near 70 and the wind easing by Thursday. Long sleeves and a windbreaker today, a jacket after sunset.

Beach & Weather

Sunny all day, high near 74, after a 48 degree dawn; the north wind runs around 14 mph with gusts to 22, strongest through the morning and easing a little after dark. Tonight clear, low around 52, north wind around 11 to 13 mph gusting 21. Surf 3 to 5 feet on a 7 foot east swell, rip current risk Moderate until 8 PM, water around 70 in the surf zone and 72 at the buoy, UV index Moderate. The only alert is the Small Craft Advisory on the coastal waters through Wednesday evening. Long sleeves and a windbreaker for the beach, and a real layer at dawn and after sunset at 6:29 PM.

Wednesday is a repeat, sunny and 72 with the north wind 11 to 14 mph gusting 21, surf 3 to 5 feet and Moderate rip risk, ocean low 10:07 AM and high 4:31 PM. Thursday sunny and 70, north wind 8 to 10 mph, surf 2 to 4 feet. Friday sunny and 70 on a northeast wind, surf 1 to 3 feet, the calmest water of the week. Saturday sunny and 67 with the northeast wind back to around 15 mph, surf 2 to 5 feet and the rip current risk High. Sunday sunny and 70. No rain in the seven day forecast at all.

Things To Do

  • Shells and shark teeth at the 9:08 AM low tide. The beach-side low is 9:08 AM today (the evening low, 10:06 PM, is in the dark). The weekend's gale and today's 7 foot swell have stirred the bottom, and new finds follow disturbed sand; look along the tideline for small black triangles. The wind is from the north, so walk northeast into it first and come home with it at your back. Kids love this one; bring a sifter if you have one. Tomorrow's beach low is 10:07 AM.
  • Walk Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S. Shore Dr, Surf City. Open 24 hours; the grill and ice cream window runs 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM on a Tuesday (pier site, read today), and a spectator ticket is $1 for anyone 11 and older (fees page, October 14). With a 7 foot swell running, the end of the pier is the best seat on the island for watching the ocean work. Good with kids for an hour.
  • Lunch in Surf City. SurfDog Bites & Brews, 204-A N. New River Dr in Treasure Coast Square, is open 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM on Tuesdays (own site, read today): burgers, bowls, hot dogs and salads a block off the beach, easy with kids. The Patio at the Surf, 104 Charlie Medlin Dr by the roundabout, serves breakfast, brunch and lunch 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM Monday to Saturday (own site, read today). Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave on the waterway, is open seven days from 8:00 AM with breakfast until 10:30 AM (own site, read today). Note for Tuesday: Max's Pizza and The Beach Shop & Grill are closed Tuesdays (own sites, October 16), and Daddy Mac's own site disagrees with itself about Tuesdays, so call 910-328-5577 first.
  • Browse the south end, Topsail Beach. South End Outfitters, 627 S. Anderson Blvd, is open 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Tuesdays (own site, read today), and the Operation Bumblebee observation towers stand along Anderson Blvd on the drive down, with the state marker at Flake Ave; some are private homes now, so look from the road. White Crab Coffee and Books, 708 S. Anderson Blvd, publishes no hours, so call ahead or take your chances.
  • Sunset over the sound at 6:29 PM. The beach faces the sunrise, so the sunset is on the sound side: the boardwalk at Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City (hours not published by the town), or in North Topsail Beach the observation deck at W. Rodney Knowles Park, 3623 Island Dr, open 8 AM to dusk year round with paid parking (town parks page, October 16). The north wind will be in your face there, so bring the jacket.
  • Dinner in North Topsail Beach. Beach Bums Pizzeria, Bar & Grill, 4021 Island Dr, is open 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM on Tuesdays (own site, read today), pizza and a family room. No rain is forecast this week, so no indoor fallback is needed; if the wind drives you off the beach, the Surf City shop strip on N. New River Dr (fudge at Topsail Island Trading Company, popcorn, candy) is a short walk, hours not checked.

Fish Friday's morning ebb before the storms, then a cool clear weekend for trout

Friday is warm, 76, with a south wind and thunderstorms by afternoon; a front then clears Saturday to sunny, 71 and a north breeze. Fish the sound's morning ebb Friday before the rain, then the creeks and piers in the cool, clear weekend.

The Bite

Best bet: The sound on Friday's morning ebb, 5:32 AM high to the 12:02 PM low, live shrimp under a slip cork in 4 to 8 foot creek seams for trout, ahead of the storms.

The sound

  • WhenFriday ebb 5:32 AM to 12:02 PM, flood to 5:49 PM in rising wind and rain. Saturday ebb 6:20 AM to 12:54 PM, sunny, north wind.
  • WhereCreek seams and deeper holes off Banks Channel for trout; oyster banks and docks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp was closed this week; check first.
  • BaitLive shrimp under a cork, or a 3/16 oz jig head and soft plastic; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout plentiful, some to 28 inches, black drum to 18 on floated shrimp (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The inlet

  • WhereWalk in through the Topsail Nature Preserve, dogs leashed.
  • LatestFalse albacore and bluefish on bait nearshore, Spanish fading (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFriday ocean low 11:10 AM, high 5:11 PM; Saturday low 11:58 AM. Seas 3 to 4 feet.
  • BaitSand fleas or shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig.
  • LatestOccasional specks; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy reporting area (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues at first light, bottom fish after dark. The Fall Challenge fishes Friday midnight to Sunday noon: crowds, no pin rigs.
  • WhereSurf City Pier, open 24 hours, no licence needed; call Jolly Roger and Seaview.
  • BaitBloodworms or shrimp on a bottom rig; Gotcha plugs for blues.
  • LatestReds 32 inches and up on the north-end piers, pompano and black drum at Surf City (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

Set the slip cork's stopper knot so the shrimp hangs a foot off the bottom; hook it through the horn so it stays lively.

Beach & Weather

  • TodayFriday starts warm, 76 and humid, with showers and thunderstorms moving in by afternoon; south wind 5 to 12 with gusts to 20. Rain a tenth to a quarter inch.
  • TonightStorms likely Friday evening, then clearing as the wind swings north; low 57. Saturday sunny, 71, north wind 10 to 15 knots; Sunday sunny, 66.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet, waves to 3.6 at the beach, water 69. NWS stopped rating rip currents for the season, so wade knee deep and watch the sideways pull.
  • WearRain jacket Friday, fleece and long pants for Saturday's north wind and a 45 degree night, sunscreen all weekend.

Regs Corner

  • LicenceAnyone 16 or older needs a Coastal Recreational Fishing License for the surf, inlet or sound: 10 days, $9 resident, $15 nonresident, online at Go Outdoors NC. Surf City and Jolly Roger piers' blanket licences cover you on the planks.
  • Red drum18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
  • Speckled trout14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one of them may be over 26.
  • FlounderClosed; release every one.
  • New from NCDMFNothing recreational; the newest proclamation (Oct 30) is a commercial croaker closure. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure, so oysters are open.

The Last Cast

Friday's thunderstorms are the one thing on the island that will show up exactly when promised. The fish keep their own calendar.

## Text
Arrival day fishes late: showers and thunderstorms through the afternoon on a south wind gusting near 30, then it clears after 9 PM and the wind swings north. Walk out on a pier after dark on the falling tide with shrimp on a bottom rig for spot and sea mullet; the Fall Challenge starts fishing at midnight. Saturday is sunny, 70, with a north wind. Pack the rain jacket on top; the bluefish will not wait for you to unpack.

## Lead
Friday is warm and wet: showers from mid morning, thunderstorms through the afternoon and evening on a gusty south wind, then clearing after dark. Fish a pier tonight once the storms pass, and the creeks and surf in Saturday's sun.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** A pier tonight after the storms clear, on the ebb from the 5:11 PM ocean high, shrimp or bloodworms on a bottom rig for spot and sea mullet.

### The sound
- **When:** Today's flood, 12:02 PM to 5:49 PM, brings gusts near 30 and thunderstorms; wait for Saturday's sunny ebb, 6:20 AM to 12:54 PM.
- **Where:** Creek seams 4 to 8 feet deep off Banks Channel for trout; oyster banks and docks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp may still be closed; call first.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a slip cork, or a 3/16 oz jig and plastic; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches; black drum to 18 on floated shrimp; reds best on cut bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **Latest:** Good-sized bluefish just off the beach, false albacore possible on surface bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Seas 3 to 4 feet today; better Saturday, low 11:58 AM, in sun and a north wind.
- **Bait:** Sand fleas or fresh shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig.
- **Latest:** Occasional specks here; slot reds, whiting and black drum at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** After dark, once the rain clears; the Fall Challenge starts at midnight. Surf City Pier is open 24 hours; Jolly Roger $16 a day, kids half price; Seaview call 910-328-3172.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or bloodworms on the bottom; a Gotcha plug at first light for blues.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger: scattered specks, bottom fish better at night, blues early. Surf City: pompano, black drum, croaker. Reds over 32 inches up north (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
East Coast Sports, 409 Roland Ave, has bait until 8 PM tonight. Buy fresh shrimp and thread it on so the hook point stays bare.

## Beach & Weather
- **Today:** Mostly cloudy and humid, 75, showers from mid morning and thunderstorms likely from early afternoon; south wind 7 to 12, gusts to 21, more in the storms. Up to a half inch of rain.
- **Tonight:** Storms likely until about 9 PM, then clearing; low 57, wind turning north. Saturday sunny, 70, north wind 12 gusting 18, and a 44 degree night.
- **Surf:** Seas 3 to 4 feet, waves to 3.5 at the beach, water 69. NWS no longer rates rip currents for the season, so wade knee deep and watch for sideways pull.
- **Wear:** Rain jacket today, then fleece and long pants for Saturday's north wind.

## Regs Corner
- **Licence:** Anyone 16 or older needs a Coastal Recreational Fishing License for surf, inlet or sound: 10 days, $9 resident, $15 nonresident, online at Go Outdoors NC. Surf City and Jolly Roger piers' blanket licences cover you on the planks.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one may be over 26.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational; Thursday's proclamation covers commercial oyster dredging from Nov 16. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure this morning, but today's rain can bring one; check the list before raking oysters.

- **Rainy afternoon, Hampstead Library.** 75 Library Dr, Hampstead, open 10 AM to 6 PM today, free, dry and quiet, with a children's area; about 20 minutes off the island.
- **Walk Surf City Pier after the rain.** 112 S Shore Dr, open 24 hours, a dollar to walk out; grill and ice cream to midnight tonight. The fall tournament weighs in there all weekend, so there are fish to see. Kids fine.
- **Saturday's beach.** Low tide 11:58 AM in sun and a north wind: walk southwest with the wind at your back, eyes on the tide line for black shark teeth. Breakfast first at Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave, 8 to 10:30 AM, seven days.

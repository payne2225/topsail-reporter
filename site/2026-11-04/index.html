East breeze, easy sound: float a shrimp for trout on the afternoon flood

Sunny, 76, east wind under 10 and seas of 3 to 4 feet: the sound fishes best. Trout are schooling in the creeks and the piers hold over-slot reds. Float a shrimp on the afternoon flood, then a pier at first light for blues.

NumbersThe BiteBeachRegsWeek

The Bite

Best bet: The sound on the afternoon flood, 10:14 AM low to the 4:07 PM high at Hampstead, live shrimp under a slip cork in the deeper creek seams for speckled trout.

The sound

  • WhenEbb to the 10:14 AM low, then flood to 4:07 PM. Neap tide, east wind 6 to 9 mph.
  • WhereCreek seams 4 to 8 feet deep off Banks Channel for trout; docks and oyster banks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp is closed this week.
  • BaitLive shrimp under a cork, or a 3/16 oz jig head with a soft plastic; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestLots of small trout, some to 28 inches, plus black drum and small reds (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The inlet

  • WhereWalk in through the Topsail Nature Preserve, dogs leashed. Boats: sight-cast surface feeds outside for false albacore.
  • LatestBlues and Spanish slowed with the wind; albies nearshore (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenOcean low 9:16 AM, high 3:31 PM. An east swell of 4 feet at 8 seconds makes it sloppy; sand fleas or shrimp on a bottom rig.
  • LatestThe occasional speckled trout here; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy reporting area (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBluefish thick at first light; bottom fishing better after dark.
  • WhereSurf City Pier is open 24 hours, no licence needed; call Jolly Roger and Seaview first.
  • BaitBloodworms or shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig; Gotcha plugs for blues.
  • LatestReds over 32 inches on the north-end piers, pompano and black drum at Surf City (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Those reds go back; the slot ends at 27.

Tip

Slide the slip cork's stopper knot up the line until the shrimp hangs a foot off the bottom.

Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny, high 76. East wind 6 to 9 mph with gusts to 17 around midday, easing to 7 by evening.
  • TonightLow 65, partly cloudy, patchy fog late; fog lingers past sunrise Thursday.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet from the east, waves to 3.4 feet at the beach, water 69. NWS stopped rating rip currents for the season on Oct 31, so wade knee-deep only and watch the sideways pull.
  • WearShorts and a light long sleeve at dawn, sunscreen by midday, a windbreaker on the pier after dark.

Regs Corner

  • Red drum18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
  • Speckled trout14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one of them may be over 26.
  • FlounderClosed; release every one.
  • New from NCDMFNothing recreational since Oct 23. FF-49-2026 (Oct 30) is a commercial croaker closure. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure this morning, so oysters are open.

Parting Shot

The bluefish are up before you are. Bring a wire leader and modest hopes for your bait bucket.

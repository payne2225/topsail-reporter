The Bite

Best bet: The marsh drains north of the inlet from 12:50 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 4:28 PM sound low, cut mullet on a Carolina rig for reds, in a creek bend out of the wind.

The sound

  • WhenFlood to the 9:56 AM high, ebb to the 4:28 PM low. Out of the grass 12:50 PM to 7:54 PM; dawn is the calmer window.
  • WhereCreek mouths and deep seams off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster banks once the marsh empties.
  • BaitLive shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in 4 to 8 feet for trout; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The inlet

  • WhenEbb from the 9:11 AM inlet high to the 4:28 PM low, on foot from the Point; seas 4 to 6 feet keep boats home.
  • LatestBluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait, sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFlood to the 8:54 AM high, ebb to the 3:26 PM low. Waves to 3 feet, water 68.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano.
  • LatestSlot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues early, bottom fish after dark; no pier has posted a closing date.
  • LatestJolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

A wind on your casting shoulder wraps a cork rig around the rod tip. Turn so it blows across the other one, cast low, and let the current do the rest.

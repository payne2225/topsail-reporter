Sunny, 72 and barely a breeze: shells at the 2:05 PM low, a bike ride, owls at dusk

Sunny, 72 and nearly windless: the best beach day left in the week, with Thursday's blow and Friday's chill still ahead. Low tide for shells is 2:05 PM and the sun sets over the sound at 5:09 PM.

NumbersBeachWeekTo DoAroundEat

Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny and 72 after a 48 degree start; a north wind near 6 at dawn, then a south sea breeze under 10 by afternoon.
  • TonightMostly cloudy and 53, a light west wind. Wednesday mostly sunny and 72; Thursday southwest 20 to 25 knots on the water with gusts to 30, seas 3 to 5 feet.
  • SurfSeas 2 feet on an east swell at 11 seconds, water 68. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; a new moon low still pulls hard off the bars, so wade knee deep.
  • WearFleece at dawn, shirtsleeves by late morning, sunglasses: flat water and a high sun.

Things To Do

  • Shells at the 2:05 PM low. A new moon low still bares the wide sand; walk the tide line for black triangles. The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is quiet on a Tuesday; dogs leashed there. Kids love it.
  • Coffee on the south end. White Crab Coffee and Books, 708 S Anderson Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM today, books and baked goods. The Patio at the Surf, by the roundabout, serves breakfast from 8 AM; call ahead for an earlier opener.
  • Ride with no headwind. 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S Topsail Dr, Surf City, 10 AM to 6 PM, e-bikes and cruisers; Herring's, 701 N New River Dr, 9 AM to 8 PM, cruiser $20 for four hours. Wind under 10 all day.
  • A pier at sunrise. Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S Shore Dr, open 24 hours, a dollar to walk out; the sun comes up over the ocean at 6:39 AM and the grill pours coffee from 7.
  • Owls at dusk. Hammocks Beach State Park, Swansboro, 5:30 to 6:30 PM, meet on the visitor center back deck; bring a flashlight. Weather dependent, and tonight is dry. About 70 minutes' drive.
  • Wilmington indoors. Cape Fear Museum, 230 Grace St, reopens today 9 AM to 5 PM, $16 adults, $10 ages 3 to 17; Battleship North Carolina daily 8 AM to 5 PM, $14 adults, $6 ages 6 to 11. About 50 minutes.

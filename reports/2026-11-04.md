## Text
The sound is the play today: float live shrimp under a cork in the creek seams on the flood from the 10:14 AM low to the 4:07 PM high, with cut mullet on the banks for reds. An east wind under 10 keeps the surf sloppy but fishable. The north-end piers put up 32-inch reds in Sunday's Fisherman's Post, every one too big to keep. Bluefish are thick at first light, and so are the bait thieves.

## Lead
Sunny, 76, east wind under 10 and seas of 3 to 4 feet: the sound fishes best. Trout are schooling in the creeks and the piers hold over-slot reds. Float a shrimp on the afternoon flood, then a pier at first light for blues.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The sound on the afternoon flood, 10:14 AM low to the 4:07 PM high at Hampstead, live shrimp under a slip cork in the deeper creek seams for speckled trout.

### The sound
- **When:** Ebb to the 10:14 AM low, then flood to 4:07 PM. Neap tide, east wind 6 to 9 mph.
- **Where:** Creek seams 4 to 8 feet deep off Banks Channel for trout; docks and oyster banks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp is closed this week.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a cork, or a 3/16 oz jig head with a soft plastic; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Lots of small trout, some to 28 inches, plus black drum and small reds (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **Where:** Walk in through the Topsail Nature Preserve, dogs leashed. Boats: sight-cast surface feeds outside for false albacore.
- **Latest:** Blues and Spanish slowed with the wind; albies nearshore (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Ocean low 9:16 AM, high 3:31 PM. An east swell of 4 feet at 8 seconds makes it sloppy; sand fleas or shrimp on a bottom rig.
- **Latest:** The occasional speckled trout here; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy reporting area (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Bluefish thick at first light; bottom fishing better after dark.
- **Where:** Surf City Pier is open 24 hours, no licence needed; call Jolly Roger and Seaview first.
- **Bait:** Bloodworms or shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig; Gotcha plugs for blues.
- **Latest:** Reds over 32 inches on the north-end piers, pompano and black drum at Surf City (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Those reds go back; the slot ends at 27.

### Tip
Slide the slip cork's stopper knot up the line until the shrimp hangs a foot off the bottom.

## Beach & Weather
- **Today:** Sunny, high 76. East wind 6 to 9 mph with gusts to 17 around midday, easing to 7 by evening.
- **Tonight:** Low 65, partly cloudy, patchy fog late; fog lingers past sunrise Thursday.
- **Surf:** Seas 3 to 4 feet from the east, waves to 3.4 feet at the beach, water 69. NWS stopped rating rip currents for the season on Oct 31, so wade knee-deep only and watch the sideways pull.
- **Wear:** Shorts and a light long sleeve at dawn, sunscreen by midday, a windbreaker on the pier after dark.

## Regs Corner
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one of them may be over 26.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational since Oct 23. FF-49-2026 (Oct 30) is a commercial croaker closure. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure this morning, so oysters are open.

## Parting Shot
The bluefish are up before you are. Bring a wire leader and modest hopes for your bait bucket.

## Island Lead
A sunny 76 with a light east breeze: the beach day of the week, with low tide at 9:16 AM for shells and a sound-side sunset at 5:14 PM. Friday turns wet, so take this one outside.

## Things To Do
- **Shell the low tide.** Beach low tide is 9:16 AM: walk the tide line for shells and small black shark teeth. Kids love it; start at any Surf City access, with restrooms at accesses 5, 15, 18 and 19.
- **Walk Surf City Pier.** 112 S Shore Dr, open 24 hours, a dollar to walk out; the grill and ice cream window run 7 AM to 10 PM. Pelicans and the bluefish crowd at first light. Kids fine; hold hands at the rail.
- **Breakfast on the water.** Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave, serves breakfast 8 to 10:30 AM on the ICW docks, seven days a week. The Patio at the Surf by the roundabout is open 8 AM to 3 PM too.
- **Ride the island.** 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S Topsail Dr, rents beach cruisers, e-bikes and paddleboards, open 10 AM to 6 PM. The sound side sits in the lee of an east wind.
- **Mine for gems.** Surf City Rocks, 22270 US 17 in Hampstead, 10 AM to 6 PM: buy a bucket, sift it in the outdoor trough, keep what you find, a geode in every bucket. About 20 minutes off the island, built for kids.
- **Sunset over the sound.** Sun down 5:14 PM: the Soundside Park boardwalk, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, looks west across the ICW. Free parking; the 4:07 PM high leaves the marsh full.

## Text
Friday on Topsail opens with an ebb in the sound from the 5:32 AM high to the 12:02 PM low, and showers with thunder follow in the afternoon, so fish the morning: live shrimp under a cork in the creek seams for trout. Saturday clears behind the front with a north wind and a 45 degree night, and Sunday is calm and sunny. The piers put up reds over 32 inches last week, every one too big to keep. Pack a rain jacket and a wire leader; the bluefish have not read the forecast.

## Lead
Friday is warm, 76, with a south wind and thunderstorms by afternoon; a front then clears Saturday to sunny, 71 and a north breeze. Fish the sound's morning ebb Friday before the rain, then the creeks and piers in the cool, clear weekend.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The sound on Friday's morning ebb, 5:32 AM high to the 12:02 PM low, live shrimp under a slip cork in 4 to 8 foot creek seams for trout, ahead of the storms.

### The sound
- **When:** Friday ebb 5:32 AM to 12:02 PM, flood to 5:49 PM in rising wind and rain. Saturday ebb 6:20 AM to 12:54 PM, sunny, north wind.
- **Where:** Creek seams and deeper holes off Banks Channel for trout; oyster banks and docks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp was closed this week; check first.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a cork, or a 3/16 oz jig head and soft plastic; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout plentiful, some to 28 inches, black drum to 18 on floated shrimp (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **Where:** Walk in through the Topsail Nature Preserve, dogs leashed.
- **Latest:** False albacore and bluefish on bait nearshore, Spanish fading (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Friday ocean low 11:10 AM, high 5:11 PM; Saturday low 11:58 AM. Seas 3 to 4 feet.
- **Bait:** Sand fleas or shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig.
- **Latest:** Occasional specks; slot reds and whiting at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy reporting area (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark. The Fall Challenge fishes Friday midnight to Sunday noon: crowds, no pin rigs.
- **Where:** Surf City Pier, open 24 hours, no licence needed; call Jolly Roger and Seaview.
- **Bait:** Bloodworms or shrimp on a bottom rig; Gotcha plugs for blues.
- **Latest:** Reds 32 inches and up on the north-end piers, pompano and black drum at Surf City (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
Set the slip cork's stopper knot so the shrimp hangs a foot off the bottom; hook it through the horn so it stays lively.

## Beach & Weather
- **Today:** Friday starts warm, 76 and humid, with showers and thunderstorms moving in by afternoon; south wind 5 to 12 with gusts to 20. Rain a tenth to a quarter inch.
- **Tonight:** Storms likely Friday evening, then clearing as the wind swings north; low 57. Saturday sunny, 71, north wind 10 to 15 knots; Sunday sunny, 66.
- **Surf:** Seas 3 to 4 feet, waves to 3.6 at the beach, water 69. NWS stopped rating rip currents for the season, so wade knee deep and watch the sideways pull.
- **Wear:** Rain jacket Friday, fleece and long pants for Saturday's north wind and a 45 degree night, sunscreen all weekend.

## Regs Corner
- **Licence:** Anyone 16 or older needs a Coastal Recreational Fishing License for the surf, inlet or sound: 10 days, $9 resident, $15 nonresident, online at Go Outdoors NC. Surf City and Jolly Roger piers' blanket licences cover you on the planks.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one of them may be over 26.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational; the newest proclamation (Oct 30) is a commercial croaker closure. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure, so oysters are open.

The Bite

Best bet: Topwater over the creek mouths off Banks Channel at first light to the 8:31 AM sound high, then the marsh drains from 11:33 AM, when the water leaves the grass, with Gulp or cut mullet for reds.

The sound

  • WhenHigh 8:31 AM, ebb to the 3:11 PM low, flood to 8:45 PM. Out of the grass 11:33 AM to 6:23 PM; a big new moon swing.
  • WhereCreek mouths on the flood; drains and oyster banks north of the inlet as the marsh empties; seams 4 to 8 feet deep for trout.
  • BaitA Spook Jr. early; live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in the seams; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

The inlet

  • WhenEbb from the 7:54 AM inlet high to the 3:07 PM low; the week's lightest wind, so watch for birds.
  • LatestBluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait; sight-cast a diamond or epoxy jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFalling to the 2:05 PM low, flood to 7:54 PM; a 2 foot east swell, water 68.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha early for blues.
  • LatestSlot reds, whiting and pompano on sand fleas at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues early, bottom fish after dark.
  • LatestJolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

Walk a topwater on slack line: twitch the rod tip down, reel up, twitch again. When a trout misses, keep it walking; the second strike usually sticks.

Rain jacket Friday, fleece Saturday: a wet arrival, then a sunny cool weekend

Friday is a rain-jacket day, 76 with showers and thunderstorms, and the weekend behind it is the payoff: sunny, 71 Saturday and 66 Sunday, with a north wind and nights in the mid 40s. Pack layers, and plan the beach for Saturday's late-morning low tide.

Beach & Weather

  • TodayFriday starts warm, 76 and humid, with showers and thunderstorms moving in by afternoon; south wind 5 to 12 with gusts to 20. Rain a tenth to a quarter inch.
  • TonightStorms likely Friday evening, then clearing as the wind swings north; low 57. Saturday sunny, 71, north wind 10 to 15 knots; Sunday sunny, 66.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet, waves to 3.6 at the beach, water 69. NWS stopped rating rip currents for the season, so wade knee deep and watch the sideways pull.
  • WearRain jacket Friday, fleece and long pants for Saturday's north wind and a 45 degree night, sunscreen all weekend.

Things To Do

  • Spot Festival. Sat 9 AM through Sun 6 PM, 14221 US 17, Hampstead, about 20 minutes off the island: $5, kids 6 and under free, spot dinner $12, inflatables $20 all day. No coolers or pets; bring chairs. Saturday is the sunny day.
  • Shell Saturday's low tide. Beach low 11:58 AM Saturday, 12:43 PM Sunday, in sun and a north wind: walk southwest with the wind at your back, eyes on the tide line for small black shark teeth. Kids love it. Restrooms at Surf City access 5.
  • Celebrate Downtown, Friday. Riverfront Park, Wilmington, 5 to 8:30 PM, free: food trucks, lawn games, "Hocus Pocus" at 6:45 PM, low chairs, free parking at Nutt and Brunswick from 4 PM. Outdoors, so check the city's page if the storms linger.
  • Walk Surf City Pier. 112 S Shore Dr, open 24 hours, a dollar to walk out; grill and ice cream to midnight Friday and Saturday. The fall tournament weighs in there Saturday, so there are fish to look at. Kids fine, hands on the rail.
  • Saturday breakfast. Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave, serves breakfast 8 to 10:30 AM on the ICW docks, seven days. The Jolly Roger Pier grill in Topsail Beach serves breakfast Friday through Sunday, 7 AM to 2 PM.
  • Sunset over the sound. Saturday's sun sets 5:11 PM; the Soundside Park boardwalk, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, faces west across the ICW. Bring the fleece.

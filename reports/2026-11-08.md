## Text
The wind finally quits: sunny, 66 and nearly calm by noon after a 44 degree start. Work topwater over the creeks off Banks Channel from first light through the 7:05 AM sound high, then shrimp under a cork in the deeper seams as the tide falls. Tournament fishing ends at noon, awards at 2. Seas still run 3 to 4 feet, so wade with respect. Calm water means the fish can finally see your lure, and your knots.

## Lead
Sunday is the calm after two blowy days: sunny, 66, north breeze going slack by midday and a clear 48 degree night. The spring tide tops out in the sound at 7:05 AM, so fish the creeks at dawn and the falling water after.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** Topwater over the creek mouths off Banks Channel until the 7:05 AM sound high, then live shrimp under a cork in 4 to 8 foot seams on the ebb.

### The sound
- **When:** High 7:05 AM, ebb to 1:44 PM, flood to the 7:19 PM high. New moon tomorrow: hard current.
- **Where:** Creek mouths and grass lines early, deeper seams as the sun climbs, docks on the dusk flood for reds. Bush Marina's ramp: call 910-328-2746 first.
- **Bait:** A Spook Jr. at dawn, then live shrimp under a slip cork or a 3/16 oz jig and plastic; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; black drum to 18; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **When:** Ebb from the 6:20 AM ocean high; calm and sunny, so watch for surface bait.
- **Latest:** Good bluefish off the beach, false albacore possible on sight-cast jigs (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Falling to the 12:43 PM low, flood to 6:36 PM; seas 3 to 4 feet, cleaner as the wind goes northwest.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook bottom rig for sea mullet, pompano and black drum; a Gotcha plug for blues.
- **Latest:** Occasional specks here; slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark. Tournament lines out at noon.
- **Bait:** Bloodworms or shrimp on the bottom; Gotcha plugs early.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, night mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
When a trout swirls behind your topwater and misses, keep walking it; stopping the plug ends the chase. Keep a second rod rigged with shrimp for the same spot.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational. SF-2-2026 (Nov 5) covers commercial mechanical oyster harvest from Nov 16. No new shellfish closure since Oct 16; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries. Hotline 252-515-5656.

## Island Text
Sunday is the island at its calmest: sunny, 66, the north breeze fading to nothing by lunch and a clear night in the 40s. Low tide at 12:43 PM is a big one, so the beach is wide and the shells are out. Spot plates and bands in Hampstead, a kite festival at Fort Fisher, or just a chair in the sun. Even the wind took the day off.

## Island Lead
The wind is gone: sunny, 66, nearly calm by midday and a clear night in the 40s. The 12:43 PM low tide is the best shelling hour of the week, and Hampstead's Spot Festival runs its second day.

## Things To Do
- **Shells at the 12:43 PM low.** A spring low bares the most sand of the week, and the wind is calm. Walk the tide line for black triangles; the Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is quiet. Kids love it; dogs leashed there.
- **Pier breakfast.** Jolly Roger Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM, breakfast sandwiches, $2 to walk out. Or coffee and a book at White Crab, 708 S Anderson Blvd, 7 AM to 2 PM today.
- **NC Spot Festival.** 14221 US 17, Hampstead, 9 AM to 5 PM: $5, kids 6 and under free, spot plate $12, inflatables $20 all day; Billy Yaeger 11 AM, Band of Oz 2 PM. Bring chairs; no pets or coolers. About 20 minutes.
- **Cape Fear Kite Festival.** Fort Fisher State Recreation Area, 11 AM to 4 PM, free: 100 professional flyers, live bands, food trucks, vendors, leashed dogs welcome. About 75 minutes; great for kids.
- **Onslow Veterans Pow Wow.** American Legion Fairgrounds, 146 Broadhurst Rd, Jacksonville, 11 AM to 5 PM, free; Grand Entry 1 PM. About 45 minutes; good for kids.
- **Sunset on the sound.** Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, boardwalk and pirate-ship playground, free parking; the sun drops over the water at 5:11 PM on a clear night.

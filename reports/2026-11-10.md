## The Bite
> **Best bet:** Topwater over the creek mouths off Banks Channel at first light to the 8:31 AM sound high, then the marsh drains from 11:33 AM, when the water leaves the grass, with Gulp or cut mullet for reds.

### The sound
- **When:** High 8:31 AM, ebb to the 3:11 PM low, flood to 8:45 PM. Out of the grass 11:33 AM to 6:23 PM; a big new moon swing.
- **Where:** Creek mouths on the flood; drains and oyster banks north of the inlet as the marsh empties; seams 4 to 8 feet deep for trout.
- **Bait:** A Spook Jr. early; live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in the seams; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

### The inlet
- **When:** Ebb from the 7:54 AM inlet high to the 3:07 PM low; the week's lightest wind, so watch for birds.
- **Latest:** Bluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait; sight-cast a diamond or epoxy jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Falling to the 2:05 PM low, flood to 7:54 PM; a 2 foot east swell, water 68.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha early for blues.
- **Latest:** Slot reds, whiting and pompano on sand fleas at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues early, bottom fish after dark.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
Walk a topwater on slack line: twitch the rod tip down, reel up, twitch again. When a trout misses, keep it walking; the second strike usually sticks.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **False albacore:** No size or bag limit appears in the 2026 guide or on NCDMF's false albacore page; a rule effective June 1 only tracks landings.
- **Oysters:** In season in open waters; Topsail and Stump Sounds are at normal boundaries, no new closure since Oct 16.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational since SF-2-2026 (Nov 5, commercial oyster dredging).

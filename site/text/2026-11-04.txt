Topsail Reporter, Wed Nov 4

The sound is the play today: float live shrimp under a cork in the creek seams on the flood from the 10:14 AM low to the 4:07 PM high, with cut mullet on the banks for reds. An east wind under 10 keeps the surf sloppy but fishable. The north-end piers put up 32-inch reds in Sunday's Fisherman's Post, every one too big to keep. Bluefish are thick at first light, and so are the bait thieves.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The marsh drains north of the inlet from 1:29 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 5:08 PM sound low, cut mullet on a Carolina rig for reds.

### The sound
- **When:** Flood to the 10:41 AM high, ebb to the 5:08 PM low. Out of the grass 1:29 PM to 8:42 PM.
- **Where:** Deep seams, 5 to 8 feet, in the main creeks off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster bars once the marsh empties.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig with a soft plastic for trout; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer is posted.

### The inlet
- **When:** Flood to the 9:52 AM inlet high, ebb to the 5:11 PM low; seas 2 to 4 feet.
- **Latest:** Bluefish, false albacore and gray trout on nearshore bait, diamond or epoxy jigs (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Flood to the 9:35 AM high, ebb to the 4:09 PM low; offshore wind, waves near 2 feet, water 68.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for whiting and pompano.
- **Latest:** Slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark; no pier lists a closing date.
- **Latest:** Reds past 32 inches at the north end; Jolly Roger blues early, sea mullet and spot at night; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
After a cold night, trout slide into the deepest bend of a creek. Let your jig touch bottom, then lift and drop it slowly; most bites come on the fall.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **Spanish mackerel:** The commercial state-waters season closes at 8 PM tonight (FF-35-2026); the recreational limit stays 15 a day at 12 inches.
- **Oysters:** In season in open waters; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries, nothing new on the polluted-area list since Oct 16.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational; the newest proclamation is still SF-3-2026 (Nov 10), commercial oyster dredging in Pamlico Sound.

## Things To Do
- **Shells at the 4:09 PM low.** An hour before the 5:07 PM sunset, the west wind off the land keeping the ocean side calm. Black triangles on the tide line are shark teeth; the south-end preserve is open on foot, dogs leashed. Kids love it.
- **Coffee and a book on the south end.** White Crab Coffee and Books, 708 S Anderson Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM Fridays. The Jolly Roger Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, does breakfast sandwiches 7 AM to 2 PM Friday through Sunday.
- **A pier walk at sunrise.** Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S Shore Dr, is open 24 hours; the sun is up at 6:41 AM and the grill opens at 7. Spectator pass $1, ages 11 and up. Dress for 48.
- **Dig for shark teeth on the mainland.** Surf City Rocks, 22270 US 17, Hampstead, 10 AM to 6 PM: buy a bucket, sift it in the outdoor trough, keep the fossils and a geode. About 20 minutes; built for kids.
- **Free history, or art at 1.** Fort Fisher State Historic Site, Kure Beach, open 9 to 5 Fridays, free, about 75 minutes. Closer: Cameron Art Museum, Wilmington, $17 adults, $6 ages 6 to 17, Boundless tour at 1 PM.
- **Big band tonight.** The Count Basie Orchestra, Kenan Auditorium, UNCW, 7:30 PM, $25 to $45 through UNCW Arts. Or Marty Stuart and His Fabulous Superlatives, Wilson Center, 7:30 PM. About 50 minutes.

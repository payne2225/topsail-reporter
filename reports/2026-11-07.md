## Text
Day two of the Fall Challenge is sunny, 70, with a north wind gusting to 20 and a big spring tide. Fish the sound's falling water from the 6:20 AM high for trout in the creek seams, then a pier this afternoon on the flood for blues and sea mullet. Seas run 3 to 4 feet, so wade carefully. The north wind will do your casting for you; aiming is still your job.

## Lead
Saturday clears up: sunny and 70 before it slides through the afternoon, with a north wind gusting to 20 and a spring tide moving a lot of water. Fish the sound's morning ebb for trout, then a pier on the afternoon flood.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The creeks off Banks Channel on the falling tide from the 6:20 AM sound high, live shrimp under a cork in 4 to 8 feet for trout.

### The sound
- **When:** Ebb 6:20 AM to 12:54 PM, then the flood to the 6:35 PM high for dusk. Spring tide, so the current runs hard.
- **Where:** Creek seams 4 to 8 feet deep, out of the north wind; oyster banks and grass lines on the flood for reds. Bush Marina's ramp: call 910-328-2746 first.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a slip cork, or a 3/16 oz jig and plastic; cut mullet for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; black drum to 18; reds best on cut bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The inlet
- **Latest:** Good-sized bluefish off the beach, false albacore possible; sight-cast a diamond jig at surface bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Falling to the 11:58 AM low, then the flood to 5:55 PM; seas 3 to 4 feet, north wind cross-shore.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook bottom rig for sea mullet and black drum; a Gotcha plug for blues.
- **Latest:** Occasional specks here; slot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark. Weigh-ins at all three piers and East Coast Sports until noon Sunday.
- **Bait:** Shrimp or bloodworms on the bottom; Gotcha plugs early. No pin rigs in the tournament.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered specks, mullet and spot at night; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker; 32-inch reds up north (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
On a pier, fish the downwind side so the north wind carries your line away from the pilings.

## Beach & Weather
- **Today:** Sunny, 70 by midday then falling to about 64; north wind 8 to 13 with gusts to 20, so the beach feels cooler than the number.
- **Tonight:** Partly cloudy and 44, the coldest night yet. Sunday sunny and 66 with a light northwest breeze.
- **Surf:** Seas 3 to 4 feet on a northeast swell, waves to 3.4 at the beach, water 69. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; wade knee deep and respect the sideways pull on a spring tide.
- **Wear:** Fleece and a windbreaker, sunglasses. Shorts are a morning gamble the afternoon wins.

## Island Text
Saturday is the island's best day so far: sunny, 70 and breezy from the north, cooling fast after dark and into the 40s by Sunday morning. Low tide is 11:58 AM, the hour for shells and shark teeth on the beach. The NC Spot Festival runs all day in Hampstead with bands and inflatables for the kids. Bring a fleece; the sun is doing its part, the wind is not.

## Island Lead
Saturday is the day the island promised: sunny, 70, a north breeze that turns cool by afternoon, and a 44 degree night. Low tide at 11:58 AM is for shells and shark teeth; Hampstead's Spot Festival fills the afternoon.

## Things To Do
- **Shells at low tide.** Low 11:58 AM on a big spring tide bares more sand than usual. Walk southwest with the north wind mostly behind you, eyes on the tide line for black triangles. Any ocean access; kids love it.
- **Breakfast on the pier.** Jolly Roger Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM; $2 to walk out and watch tournament weigh-ins. Coffee and books at White Crab, 708 S Anderson Blvd, 7 AM to 2 PM.
- **NC Spot Festival.** 14221 US 17, Hampstead, gates 9 AM to 10:30 PM: $5, kids 6 and under free, spot dinner $12, inflatables $20 all day; bands 12:30, 3:30 and 7:30 PM. No coolers or pets; bring chairs. About 20 minutes.
- **Onslow Veterans Pow Wow.** American Legion Fairgrounds, 146 Broadhurst Rd, Jacksonville, 11 AM to 9 PM, free; Grand Entry 1 PM and 7 PM, Native vendors and tipi tours. About 45 minutes; good for kids.
- **Gem mining for kids.** Surf City Rocks, 22270 US 17, Hampstead, 10 AM to 6 PM: buy a bucket, sift it at the outdoor trough, keep what you find, a geode in every one. Pairs with the festival.
- **Scotty McCreery and Lonestar.** 417 Kraft St, Holly Ridge, 4 to 11 PM, rain or shine, tickets on Etix; no coolers or outside food, chairs under 9 inches. Bring the fleece for the 44 degree night.

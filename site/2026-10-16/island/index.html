Beach by day, festival by night in Topsail Beach, then Saturday turns cold and wet

Friday is the warm one: sunny, a high near 82, a southwest breeze gusting to 25, surf of 1 to 2 feet with a Low rip current risk, and water still 77 degrees. The Autumn With Topsail Festival opens at 6:00 PM in Topsail Beach, and the beach-side low tide at 6:30 PM is the shell and shark-tooth hour. Saturday brings the first cold front of the fall, rain likely from late morning, a north wind gusting to 35 and a high near 65 that falls through the afternoon, with Sunday cold, windy and clearer. Pack the shorts for today and the jackets for the rest of the weekend.

Things To Do

  • Autumn With Topsail Festival, tonight from 6:00 PM, Topsail Beach. Historic Assembly Building, 720 Channel Blvd (per Visit Pender). $7 Friday night, children under 12 free; a free shuttle runs from the town's paid parking lots, so park once and ride. Saturday and Sunday add over 100 artists and crafters, live music, food, a beer and wine tent and a bouncy house area for kids ($7 a day, $17 for three days). Saturday looks wet and windy, so tonight and Sunday are the better bets; check the festival site before going Saturday.
  • Shells and shark teeth at the 6:30 PM low tide. The beach-side low is 6:30 PM today, with the tide falling through the late afternoon. Walk the tideline and look for small black triangles; the evening light is kind and the wind is at your back heading south. Kids love this one. Tomorrow's beach low is 5:57 AM, before the rain, if anyone is up.
  • Walk Surf City Ocean Pier, 112 S. Shore Dr, Surf City. Open 24 hours; the grill and ice cream window runs 7 AM to midnight on Fridays (pier site, read this morning), and a spectator ticket is $1 for anyone 11 and older (fees page, October 14). A good hour for kids before dinner, with the Spanish mackerel anglers to watch.
  • Lunch or dinner in Topsail Beach: The Beach Shop & Grill, 701 S. Anderson Blvd. Lunch 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM and dinner 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM on Fridays (its own site, read this morning), a short walk from the festival grounds. Patio seating is subject to weather closure, and tonight is fine for it.
  • Breakfast or an easy family dinner in Surf City. The Patio at the Surf, 104 Charlie Medlin Dr, serves breakfast, brunch and lunch 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM Monday to Saturday and tapas 3:00 PM to 11:00 PM Friday (own site, read this morning). Sears Landing, 806 Roland Ave on the waterway, is open seven days from 8:00 AM with breakfast until 10:30 AM (own site, read this morning). Max's Pizza, 602 A Roland Ave, is open 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM Friday and Saturday (own site, read this morning), the easy call with kids.
  • Rainy-day plan for Saturday. On the island there is no museum open this weekend (Missiles and More is closed for the season). The free Discovery Bay mini aquarium at Independence Mall in Wilmington is open Saturday 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM and the Cape Fear Museum, 230 Grace St, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (both pages read October 16 for the reference file); allow extra time for IRONMAN traffic in Wilmington Saturday morning. Closer in, the Hampstead Branch Library, 75 Library Drive, is open Saturday 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Browsing the south end shops is another fallback: South End Outfitters, 627 S. Anderson Blvd, is open 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM Friday and Saturday (own site, read this morning).

Beach & Weather

Sunny and warm this afternoon, high near 82, with a southwest wind around 16 mph and gusts to 25. Tonight partly cloudy, low around 62 with temperatures rising to around 69 overnight, southwest 10 to 15 mph gusting 24. Surf is 1 to 2 feet, rip current risk Low, UV index High, water in the mid 70s in the surf zone and 77 at the buoy. No alerts on land; the Gale Warning is for the coastal waters, from Saturday afternoon through late Sunday night. Shorts and a sun shirt today, with a windbreaker for the beach late in the day.

Saturday is the first real cold front of the fall: rain showers likely, mainly late morning, mostly cloudy, a high near 65 falling to around 58 in the afternoon, north wind 13 to 23 mph with gusts to 35, then a low around 46 Saturday night with a 40 percent chance of rain. Surf stays 1 to 2 feet with Low rip risk Saturday. Sunday is mostly sunny but cold and blustery, high near 63, north wind around 23 mph gusting 35, rip current risk High, surf 2 to 4 feet. Monday the swell peaks: rip current risk High and surf 4 to 7 feet with a high near 67. Tuesday eases to Moderate rip risk, surf 2 to 4 feet, sunny and 71. Dig out the sweatshirts and a real jacket by Saturday afternoon.

Around the Island

  • Tonight, Friday 6:00 PM: the 37th Autumn With Topsail Festival opens in Topsail Beach (festival site and Visit Pender list it at the Historic Assembly Building, 720 Channel Blvd). Friday night is $7; Saturday and Sunday are $7 a day for adults, $12 for two days or $17 for all three, active military and veterans $5, children under 12 free. Over 100 regional artists and crafters, live music, food, a beer and wine tent, and a bouncy house area for kids from Saturday morning; the silent auction in the Assembly Building ends Sunday at 2:00 PM. A free shuttle runs from the paid parking lots in Topsail Beach (paid parking is enforced); no parking on South Anderson Blvd, and no pets except service dogs. The festival page carries no weather notice, but Saturday's forecast is rain and a north wind gusting to 35, so check the festival site before heading out Saturday.
  • Friday and Saturday: Pender County early voting at the Hampstead Annex, 15060 US 17, 8:00 AM to 7:30 PM Friday and 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM Saturday (county news, October 12; early voting runs through October 31).
  • Saturday: Burgaw's Autumn Fest is cancelled over weather concerns, with the races still on (WECT headline, October 16). Visit Pender still lists it; trust the cancellation.
  • Saturday: IRONMAN 70.3 North Carolina starts at Wrightsville Beach at 7:10 AM and finishes downtown Wilmington, with the bike course crossing into rural Pender County; expect delays (Port City Daily, October 15). The Heide Trask drawbridge to Wrightsville Beach is down to one lane for emergency repairs (WECT, October 15).
  • North Topsail Beach: comments on the Army Corps draft environmental impact statement for the New River Inlet management plan are due October 19 (town news, October 13).
  • The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is open after its October 7 herbicide treatment. Dogs on a leash at all times there, no beach driving or horses on the preserve strand, and foot access for anglers stays open.
  • Topsail Beach paid parking runs through October 31 (South End lot $7 an hour or $35 a day, ocean lots $6 an hour or $30 a day).
  • No road or bridge closures were posted this morning by Topsail Beach, Surf City, North Topsail Beach or Pender County. Surf City's newest notice is a September 29 parking-services request for proposals, nothing operational.

Parting Shot

The festival's silent auction sits in the Historic Assembly Building, where the Navy put together its Operation Bumblebee test missiles; the state marker two blocks east says seven of the island's eight observation towers still stand, and some of them are now somebody's beach house.

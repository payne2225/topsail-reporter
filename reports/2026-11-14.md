## Lead
A cold, clear Saturday: 42 at the 6:43 AM sunrise, sunny and 58 by afternoon, northwest 9 to 13, no advisory. The marsh is off the grass until 7:45 AM, then the flood runs to the 11:29 AM sound high.

## The Bite
> **Best bet:** The drains north of the inlet at the 6:43 AM sunrise, while the water is off the grass until 7:45 AM, cut mullet for reds, then live shrimp for trout on the flood.

### The sound
- **When:** Flood from the 4:57 AM low to the 11:29 AM high. Out of the grass until 7:45 AM, and again 2:10 PM to 9:34 PM.
- **Where:** Drains and oyster bars at dawn; the deep seams, 5 to 8 feet, in the creeks off Banks Channel on the flood.
- **Bait:** Live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig with a soft plastic for trout; cut mullet on a Carolina rig for reds.
- **Latest:** Small trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait beating plastics (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer is posted.

### The inlet
- **When:** Flood to the 10:36 AM high; north 15 to 20 knots early, seas 2 to 3 feet.
- **Latest:** Bluefish, false albacore and gray trout on nearshore structure, diamond or epoxy jigs (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The surf
- **When:** Flood to the 10:19 AM high, ebb to the 4:55 PM low; offshore wind, chop 1 to 3 feet, water 67.
- **Bait:** Shrimp on a two-hook rig for whiting; a Gotcha plug for blues at first light.
- **Latest:** Occasional trout in the surf; Spanish and blues slowed by wind (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### The piers
- **When:** Blues at first light, bottom fish after dark.
- **Latest:** Jolly Roger scattered trout, mullet and spot at night, blues early; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

### Tip
A 42 degree dawn pushes trout into the deepest bend of a creek. Fish slow: let the jig rest on bottom a count of three before each lift.

## Regs Corner
- **Speckled trout:** 14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one over 26 allowed.
- **Red drum:** 18 to 27 inches, one a day. Black drum 14 to 25, ten. Bluefish five.
- **Flounder:** Closed; release every one.
- **Oysters:** In season in open waters; Topsail and Stump Sounds at normal boundaries, nothing new on the polluted-area list since Oct 16.
- **Report it:** Any kept drum or trout goes to deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
- **New from NCDMF:** Nothing recreational; the newest proclamation is still SF-3-2026 (Nov 10), commercial oyster dredging in Pamlico Sound. The commercial Spanish mackerel season closed last night; the recreational limit is unchanged.

The island's sunny Saturday: shells at low tide, spot dinners in Hampstead

Saturday is the day the island promised: sunny, 70, a north breeze that turns cool by afternoon, and a 44 degree night. Low tide at 11:58 AM is for shells and shark teeth; Hampstead's Spot Festival fills the afternoon.

NumbersBeachWeekTo DoAroundEat

Beach & Weather

  • TodaySunny, 70 by midday then falling to about 64; north wind 8 to 13 with gusts to 20, so the beach feels cooler than the number.
  • TonightPartly cloudy and 44, the coldest night yet. Sunday sunny and 66 with a light northwest breeze.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet on a northeast swell, waves to 3.4 at the beach, water 69. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; wade knee deep and respect the sideways pull on a spring tide.
  • WearFleece and a windbreaker, sunglasses. Shorts are a morning gamble the afternoon wins.

Things To Do

  • Shells at low tide. Low 11:58 AM on a big spring tide bares more sand than usual. Walk southwest with the north wind mostly behind you, eyes on the tide line for black triangles. Any ocean access; kids love it.
  • Breakfast on the pier. Jolly Roger Grill, 803 Ocean Blvd, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM; $2 to walk out and watch tournament weigh-ins. Coffee and books at White Crab, 708 S Anderson Blvd, 7 AM to 2 PM.
  • NC Spot Festival. 14221 US 17, Hampstead, gates 9 AM to 10:30 PM: $5, kids 6 and under free, spot dinner $12, inflatables $20 all day; bands 12:30, 3:30 and 7:30 PM. No coolers or pets; bring chairs. About 20 minutes.
  • Onslow Veterans Pow Wow. American Legion Fairgrounds, 146 Broadhurst Rd, Jacksonville, 11 AM to 9 PM, free; Grand Entry 1 PM and 7 PM, Native vendors and tipi tours. About 45 minutes; good for kids.
  • Gem mining for kids. Surf City Rocks, 22270 US 17, Hampstead, 10 AM to 6 PM: buy a bucket, sift it at the outdoor trough, keep what you find, a geode in every one. Pairs with the festival.
  • Scotty McCreery and Lonestar. 417 Kraft St, Holly Ridge, 4 to 11 PM, rain or shine, tickets on Etix; no coolers or outside food, chairs under 9 inches. Bring the fleece for the 44 degree night.

Veterans Day, 72 and nearly still: shells at the 2:45 PM low, a ride, a sound sunset

Partly sunny, 72 and nearly windless on Veterans Day, the last gentle one before Thursday's blow and Friday's chill. Low tide for shells is 2:45 PM and the sun sets over the sound at 5:08 PM.

NumbersBeachWeekTo DoAroundEat

Beach & Weather

  • TodayPartly sunny and 72 after a 49 degree start; a northwest breeze near 3 at dawn, a south sea breeze under 10 by afternoon.
  • TonightPartly cloudy and 56, southwest 7 to 10 with gusts to 18. Thursday sunny and 74 but southwest 20 to 25 knots on the water, gusts to 30, seas 3 to 5 feet; Friday 61 and northwest.
  • SurfSeas 2 feet on an east swell at 11 seconds, water 68. NWS no longer rates rip currents this season; a spring low still pulls off the bars, so wade knee deep.
  • WearFleece at dawn, shirtsleeves by noon, a windbreaker for the evening drains.

Things To Do

  • Shells at the 2:45 PM low. A spring low still bares the wide sand; walk the tide line for black triangles. The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end is quiet midweek; dogs leashed there. Kids love it.
  • Coffee on the south end. White Crab Coffee and Books, Topsail Beach, 7 AM to 2 PM today, books and baked goods. The Patio at the Surf, by the roundabout, serves breakfast from 7 AM; its page also says 6, so call ahead.
  • The Battleship on Veterans Day. Battleship North Carolina, Wilmington, 8 AM to 5 PM, $14 adults, $6 ages 6 to 11, $10 military; ham radio remembrance 9 AM to 4 PM. Mostly indoors, kids roam nine levels. About 50 minutes.
  • Ride with no headwind. 50 South Surf Shop, 306 S Topsail Dr, Surf City, 10 AM to 6 PM, e-bikes and cruisers; Herring's, 701 N New River Dr, 9 AM to 8 PM, cruiser $20 for four hours. Wind under 10 all day.
  • Airlie Gardens. Bird walk 8 to 9:30 AM, then the gardens, 9 AM to 5 PM, $10 adults, $3 ages 4 to 12, open on the holiday per the county. About 45 minutes.
  • Sunset over the sound. Soundside Park, 517 Roland Ave, Surf City, free; the sun goes down at 5:08 PM over the Intracoastal. Kids get the pirate-ship playground.

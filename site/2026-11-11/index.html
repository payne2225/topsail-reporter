The Bite

Best bet: The marsh drains and oyster edges north of the inlet from 12:12 PM, when the water leaves the grass, through the 3:50 PM sound low, with cut mullet or Gulp for reds.

The sound

  • WhenHigh 9:13 AM, ebb to the 3:50 PM low, flood to 9:27 PM. Out of the grass 12:12 PM to 7:08 PM; still a big swing.
  • WhereCreek mouths off Banks Channel on the flood; the drains and oyster banks as the marsh empties; seams 4 to 8 feet for trout.
  • BaitA Spook Jr. early; live shrimp under a cork or a 3/16 oz jig in the seams; cut mullet or Gulp for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches, on floated shrimp; reds scattered, cut bait best (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1). Nothing newer online.

The inlet

  • WhenEbb from the 8:32 AM inlet high to the 3:47 PM low; light wind, so watch for birds.
  • LatestBluefish, Spanish and false albacore on bait; sight-cast a diamond jig (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenFalling to the 2:45 PM low, flood to 8:32 PM; seas 2 feet on an east swell, water 68.
  • BaitShrimp or sand fleas on a two-hook rig for sea mullet and pompano; a Gotcha early for blues.
  • LatestSlot reds, whiting and pompano at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenBlues early, bottom fish after dark; no pier has posted a closing date.
  • LatestJolly Roger scattered specks, night sea mullet and spot; Surf City pompano, black drum, croaker (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

When the water drops off the grass, reds follow it out through the little drains. Cast up-current of a drain mouth, let the cut mullet sit, and let them come to you.

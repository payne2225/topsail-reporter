Beach & Weather

Sunny and warm, high near 83, low around 66 tonight, no alerts. The wind is the story: west at 4 mph at dawn, south-southwest at 6 by midday, 10 mph mid afternoon, then southwest gusting to 18 after dark. Surf is 1 to 2 feet, rip current risk Low, and the UV index is High, so sunscreen before the fish, not after. Water is in the mid 70s in the surf zone and 77 at the buoy. Sun up 7:15 AM, down 6:37 PM. Shorts and a long sleeve sun shirt will do today.

Thursday repeats it: sunny, 84, surf about a foot, low rip risk, southwest wind around 5 mph. Friday stays sunny near 83 but the southwest wind builds to 17 mph with gusts to 25 and seas 2 to 3 feet, then it swings west and blows 20 to 25 knots overnight. Saturday is the first cold front of the fall: north wind 18 to 22 mph, a high near 69, a 40 percent chance of showers, and a low near 49 Saturday night. Sunday is northeast at 21 mph, high 66, with the rip current risk up to Moderate and surf 2 to 3 feet. Dig out a sweatshirt by the weekend.

Around the Island

  • Tonight, Wednesday 6:00 PM: Topsail Beach Town Board regular meeting at Town Hall, 820 S. Anderson Blvd, with a public hearing on a text amendment to the wireless ordinance (town site, read this morning).
  • Thursday 10:00 AM: community flu shot clinic in Topsail Beach with the Pender County Health Department (town site lists the time; check the town page for the location).
  • Thursday at sunrise: recreational oyster season opens in open waters (details in Regs Corner).
  • Thursday October 15 through October 31: early voting in Pender County for the November 3 general election, with some Election Day precinct changes (Pender County news, October 12).
  • Friday October 16 to Sunday October 18: the 37th Autumn With Topsail Festival in Topsail Beach. Friday kicks off at 6:00 PM ($7), Saturday and Sunday are $7 a day for adults or $17 for all three days, under 12 free, active military and veterans $5. Over 100 artists and craft vendors, live music, food, a beer and wine tent, kids' inflatables Saturday and Sunday, and a silent auction in the Historic Assembly Building ending Sunday at 2:00 PM. Free shuttles run from the paid parking lots in Topsail Beach.
  • North Topsail Beach: comments on the Army Corps draft environmental impact statement for the New River Inlet management plan are due October 19 (town news, October 13).
  • The Topsail Nature Preserve at the south end reopened October 7 after a one-day herbicide treatment of invasive reed. Dogs on a leash at all times, no beach driving or horses on the preserve strand, and foot access for anglers stays open.
  • Topsail Beach paid parking runs through October 31 (South End lot $7 an hour or $35 a day, ocean lots $6 an hour or $30 a day).
  • No road or bridge closures were posted this morning on the Topsail Beach, Surf City, North Topsail Beach or Pender County sites.

Parting Shot

The spot that start showing on the piers this month are the big ones: NCDMF's species profile says the larger spot move into the nearshore ocean as the fall spawn draws near. A two-hook bottom rig with a piece of bloodworm is all it takes.

Arrival day fishes after dark: storms first, then a pier bite on the ebb

Friday is warm and wet: showers from mid morning, thunderstorms through the afternoon and evening on a gusty south wind, then clearing after dark. Fish a pier tonight once the storms pass, and the creeks and surf in Saturday's sun.

The Bite

Best bet: A pier tonight after the storms clear, on the ebb from the 5:11 PM ocean high, shrimp or bloodworms on a bottom rig for spot and sea mullet.

The sound

  • WhenToday's flood, 12:02 PM to 5:49 PM, brings gusts near 30 and thunderstorms; wait for Saturday's sunny ebb, 6:20 AM to 12:54 PM.
  • WhereCreek seams 4 to 8 feet deep off Banks Channel for trout; oyster banks and docks for reds. Bush Marina's ramp may still be closed; call first.
  • BaitLive shrimp under a slip cork, or a 3/16 oz jig and plastic; cut mullet for reds.
  • LatestSmall trout thick, some to 28 inches; black drum to 18 on floated shrimp; reds best on cut bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The inlet

  • LatestGood-sized bluefish just off the beach, false albacore possible on surface bait (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The surf

  • WhenSeas 3 to 4 feet today; better Saturday, low 11:58 AM, in sun and a north wind.
  • BaitSand fleas or fresh shrimp on a two-hook bottom rig.
  • LatestOccasional specks here; slot reds, whiting and black drum at Wrightsville Beach, the nearest busy report (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

The piers

  • WhenAfter dark, once the rain clears; the Fall Challenge starts at midnight. Surf City Pier is open 24 hours; Jolly Roger $16 a day, kids half price; Seaview call 910-328-3172.
  • BaitShrimp or bloodworms on the bottom; a Gotcha plug at first light for blues.
  • LatestJolly Roger: scattered specks, bottom fish better at night, blues early. Surf City: pompano, black drum, croaker. Reds over 32 inches up north (Fisherman's Post, Nov 1).

Tip

East Coast Sports, 409 Roland Ave, has bait until 8 PM tonight. Buy fresh shrimp and thread it on so the hook point stays bare.

Beach & Weather

  • TodayMostly cloudy and humid, 75, showers from mid morning and thunderstorms likely from early afternoon; south wind 7 to 12, gusts to 21, more in the storms. Up to a half inch of rain.
  • TonightStorms likely until about 9 PM, then clearing; low 57, wind turning north. Saturday sunny, 70, north wind 12 gusting 18, and a 44 degree night.
  • SurfSeas 3 to 4 feet, waves to 3.5 at the beach, water 69. NWS no longer rates rip currents for the season, so wade knee deep and watch for sideways pull.
  • WearRain jacket today, then fleece and long pants for Saturday's north wind.

Regs Corner

  • LicenceAnyone 16 or older needs a Coastal Recreational Fishing License for surf, inlet or sound: 10 days, $9 resident, $15 nonresident, online at Go Outdoors NC. Surf City and Jolly Roger piers' blanket licences cover you on the planks.
  • Red drum18 to 27 inch slot, one a day; the 32-inch pier fish go back. Report any kept drum or trout at deq.nc.gov/report-my-fish.
  • Speckled trout14 to 20 inch slot, three a day, one may be over 26.
  • FlounderClosed; release every one.
  • New from NCDMFNothing recreational; Thursday's proclamation covers commercial oyster dredging from Nov 16. Topsail and Stump sounds show no rain closure this morning, but today's rain can bring one; check the list before raking oysters.

The Last Cast

The forecast promises thunder at the exact hour the car gets unpacked. Fish keep their own schedule, and tonight's starts after the rain.

## New Topsail Inlet / south end ("The Point", Serenity Point, now "Topsail Nature Preserve")
- The south-end tract fronting the ocean, New Topsail Inlet and Banks Channel transferred to the state; NC DEQ Division of Coastal Management manages it as the Topsail Nature Preserve with the NC Coastal Land Trust. Transfer event May 19, 2026. https://www.deq.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/05/14/deq-secretary-reid-wilson-join-staff-officials-and-conservation-partners-mark-transfer-topsail-beach
- Deed records show just over 97 acres transferred (earlier materials said ~150). Preserve "remains open for walking, fishing, beachcombing, and wildlife watching." Stay out of marked nesting areas; pack out fishing line. https://topsailguide.com/news/point-protected-topsail-beach-south-end/ (published May 20, 2026; updated July 17, 2026)
- Town notice 9/11/2026 "Protecting the South End": vehicular beach driving, horseback riding and dogs off leash no longer permitted on the preserve beach strand; beach driving and horseback permits no longer issued or renewed; dogs on leash at all times at the South End; pedestrian access stays open for anglers. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1242/Protecting-the-South-End-Updates-to-Beach-Driving,-Horseback-Riding,-and-Pet-Guidelines
- Preserve closed Oct 7, 2026 for phragmites herbicide treatment and has REOPENED (town news item dated 10/7/2026, "Topsail Nature Preserve is now OPEN!"). https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1250/Topsail-Nature-Preserve---Reopened
- Getting there: DEQ's event notice had people walk to the oceanside beach from the Sound Access #1 parking area (= the town's South End Parking Lot). Same DEQ URL.
- Inlet channel surveys (TI Coastal) posted 5/5/2026 and 9/17/2026; findings not summarized on the page. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News/ID/1243/Inlet-Channel-Survey---Summer-2026
- Bird-nesting closure dates for fall 2026: none found. UNVERIFIED (nesting season closures are normally spring/summer; nothing published for this window).
- No new preserve closure or treatment notice after Oct 7 on the town news page (read 2026-10-23); the Sept 11 rules (on foot only, dogs leashed at all times at the South End) still stand. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/News

## Topsail Beach public accesses and parking (south end)
- South End Parking Lot = Sound Access #1: paid "Premium Lot", 6:00 AM-9:00 PM, $7/hr (up to 4 hrs) or $35/day; the only lot closed 10 PM-6 AM; no overnight parking. https://topsailbeachnc.gov/Visitors/Public-Accesses-and-Parking . The 10 PM-6 AM closure is written with no seasonal limit ("This is the only lot that will be closed from 10PM to 6AM, daily", re-read 2026-10-23), so plan on it applying during the run too; night anglers at the south end cannot leave a car there.
- Ocean-front lots (Accesses #5-#15): $6/hr (up to 4 hrs) or $30/day, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM. Same URL.
- Paid parking "Enforced every day, March 1st through October 31st" (re-read 2026-10-23). The town FAQ says "Paid Parking is only in effect March 1st to October 31st between the hours of 9am and 5pm" (https://topsailbeachnc.gov/About-Topsail-Beach/FAQ , read 2026-10-23). So paid parking is NOT enforced Nov 5-14 (the pages imply it; neither says "free" in so many words). Same URL.
- Fines: $50 standard; same-day pay $30 (ocean lots) / $35 (South End lot). Same URL.
- Full list of beach and sound accesses is a linked spreadsheet ("Beach and Sound Accesses List") on the same page; not opened.
